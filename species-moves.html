Species-Specific Move Pools

This document defines the ordinary moves available to each of the 100 established creatures and the level at which each move is learned naturally.

How to read the move tables

  • Species Move Pool: The moves this species can potentially learn through its ordinary progression.
  • Level: The level at which the move becomes available through natural level progression.
  • Individual Moveset: A separate concept. An individual Entheon does not necessarily know every move in its species pool simultaneously.
  • Special Moves/Abilities: Separate from ordinary moves and do not occupy an ordinary move slot.
  • Nature/Personality: Separate from both ordinary moves and Special Moves/Abilities.

Level Progression Rules

  • Levels range from 1–100.
  • Each species begins with its earliest listed moves at Level 1.
  • Later moves unlock progressively as the individual levels.
  • Evolutionary stages receive progressively broader and more advanced learnsets.
  • The listed levels define the current canonical progression for these species.
  • The progression system tracks each individual’s current level and learned moves.
  • A newly available move does not automatically require an existing move to be forgotten; the active move-limit rules will determine how the individual manages its moveset.
  • This document defines ordinary level-up acquisition only. Other acquisition methods can be added later without changing the species’ core pool.
  • Special Moves/Abilities are intentionally excluded from these tables.
