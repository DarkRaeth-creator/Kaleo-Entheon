5. Kaleo: Geography, Regions & Travel

Overview

Kaleo is divided into ten distinct regions, each with its own terrain, climate, settlements, wildlife habitats, and local character. The regions are not uniform in size or terrain. A single region may contain several different environments, and the presence of a particular Entheon affinity does not mean every region containing that affinity has the same landscape. Kaleo’s geography is interconnected through Main Trails, Secondary Trails, and established ferry routes, allowing travel between settlements, cities, landmarks, and different regions without requiring a fixed Gym-by-Gym progression.

The Ten Regions

Westmere

Westmere is a temperate region of dense forests, wooded hills, open meadows, and scattered farmland. The forests become particularly dense toward the western and northern portions of the region, while the more settled areas contain fields, small streams, and gently rolling countryside. The region’s most famous natural feature is the Great Tree, an enormous ancient tree standing within a broad clearing surrounded by forest. Westmere is one of Kaleo’s more settled regions, with established Trails connecting its villages and Everhope City, while large stretches of woodland remain relatively wild. Major City: Everhope City Gym: Everhope Gym — Gale Settlements: Valehaven, Briarwood, Oakridge Landmark: The Great Tree

Dunridge

Dunridge is a rugged mountainous region dominated by rocky ridges, steep valleys, cliffs, caves, and narrow mountain passages. Elevation varies considerably, with relatively temperate valleys giving way to harsher terrain at higher elevations. The region forms part of the larger mountain geography separating the western and northern portions of Kaleo. Travelling through Dunridge often means following valleys, crossing rocky passes, or winding around mountain slopes rather than travelling in straight lines. The higher elevations become increasingly cold and eventually transition toward the mountainous environments of Highreach and the colder northern regions. Major City: Stonehaven Gym: None Settlements: Pinewatch, Stonebrook Landmark: None; the mountainous landscape itself defines the region.

Seawick

Seawick is a long, narrow coastal region, stretching considerably farther north-to-south than it does inland. Its geography is dominated by coastline: beaches, rocky shores, coastal cliffs, small bays, sea caves, and scattered coastal wetlands. The interior is comparatively narrow, meaning that many settlements and much of the region’s transportation infrastructure naturally follow the coastline. The region has a strong maritime character. Fishing, shipping, coastal trade, and travel along the Main Trail are important parts of everyday life. Because of its elongated geography, travelling through Seawick can mean following the coast for considerable distances before reaching the next major settlement or regional connection. Major City: Gullhaven Gym: Gullhaven Gym — Tide Settlements: Tidewatch, Driftwood Landmark: None; Seawick’s distinctive coastline defines the region.

Highreach

Highreach is a mountainous northern region characterized by steep peaks, alpine valleys, glaciers, snow-covered slopes, and high-altitude wilderness. The southern portions of the region are comparatively accessible, but the terrain becomes progressively colder and more difficult toward the north. Mountain passes and valleys provide the principal natural travel corridors. Near the northern meeting point of Highreach, Dunridge, and Northvale lies a geothermal area containing the famous Highreach Hot Springs. The springs remain warm despite the surrounding mountainous and snowy environment. Major City: Thermalis Gym: Thermalis Gym — Fire/Stone Settlements: Frostmere, Snowfall Landmark: Highreach Hot Springs

Greenvale

Greenvale is Kaleo’s major agricultural heartland. It consists primarily of broad fertile plains, gently rolling farmland, grasslands, cultivated fields, streams, and small wooded areas. Much of Greenvale has relatively open sightlines, with settlements and farms spread throughout the countryside. The region’s most recognizable structure is the Great Windmill, an enormous functioning agricultural processing facility. Its size makes it visible from considerable distances across the surrounding farmland. Major City: Harveston Gym: Harveston Gym — Verdant Settlements: Millbrook, Meadowfield, Sunvale Landmark: The Great Windmill

Lume

Lume is a relatively small island positioned within the central maritime passage between the major eastern and western portions of Kaleo. The island contains a mixture of coastal terrain, forest, rocky areas, beaches, and undeveloped wilderness. Much of Lume remains natural Entheon habitat rather than being densely developed by humans. Lume Port, the island’s major city, is the primary human population centre and is built around a naturally useful harbor. Lume’s importance comes less from its size and more from its strategic position. Established ferry connections make it an important stopping point for travellers, merchants, and cargo moving between distant parts of Kaleo. Major City: Lume Port Gym: None Settlements: None beyond Lume Port Landmark: Lume Port and the surrounding island environment

Hawthorne

Hawthorne is dominated by the enormous Great Hawthorne Lake, which forms a major geographic feature of the region. The surrounding landscape consists of forests, freshwater wetlands, rolling hills, streams, and fertile areas influenced by the lake. Rivers and waterways branch away from the lake, creating numerous smaller aquatic habitats. Lakecrest City sits directly adjacent to the lake and has developed around the opportunities provided by its freshwater access. The lake also influences travel through the region: Trails naturally follow its shoreline or pass around sections of the water rather than simply cutting directly across it. Major City: Lakecrest City Gym: Lakecrest Gym — Volt Settlements: Stillwater, Brookmere, Riverside Landmark: Great Hawthorne Lake

Northvale

Northvale is a cool northern region of rocky terrain, coniferous forests, hills, river valleys, and increasingly cold landscapes toward its northern edges. One of its defining geographical features is the Cascading River, where water travels through a series of rocky drops, pools, and smaller waterfalls, creating a natural staircase through part of the region. The southern portions of Northvale remain relatively accessible, while the northern terrain becomes colder and more rugged as it approaches the harsher environments around Isen. Northvale therefore acts as an environmental transition between the temperate regions farther south and Kaleo’s genuinely frozen northern wilderness. Major City: Northreach Gym: Northreach Gym — Metal/Frost Settlements: Stonefall, Rivermarch, Frostbridge Landmark: The Cascading River

Eastmere

Eastmere is a broad eastern region with a mixture of coastal plains, forests, grasslands, rolling countryside, rivers, and sheltered bays. The eastern coastline provides numerous natural harbors and coastal environments, while the interior becomes more varied and wooded. Fairhaven is the region’s major urban centre and is connected to the surrounding settlements by established Trails. Eastmere is relatively accessible compared with the mountainous north and west, although its more remote forests and coastal wilderness contain areas that remain largely undeveloped. Major City: Fairhaven Gym: Fairhaven Gym — Mystic Settlements: Seabrook, Windmere Landmark: None; the region’s varied eastern landscape defines it.

Isen

Isen is Kaleo’s most extreme northern environment: a vast frozen tundra characterized by permafrost, snowfields, frozen lakes and rivers, barren plains, ice-covered terrain, and long stretches of wilderness. Vegetation is sparse compared with the southern regions, and permanent human settlement is extremely limited. Winterhold is the region’s major city, with no smaller settlements currently established. Weather and terrain make travel considerably more difficult than in most of Kaleo. Established Trails connect the inhabited areas, but large portions of Isen remain wild and sparsely explored. Isen is not simply a colder version of Highreach: Highreach is mountainous and alpine, while Isen is dominated by expansive frozen tundra. Major City: Winterhold Gym: None Settlements: None Landmark: The Frozen Expanse

Major Landmarks

The Great Tree — Westmere

Deep within one of Westmere’s largest forests stands an enormous ancient tree surrounded by a broad natural clearing. The forest grows densely again beyond the clearing, making the tree and its open surroundings highly distinctive. The Great Tree is one of Westmere’s most recognizable natural landmarks and can be reached by a Secondary Trail branching from the region’s main travel network. The tree is not inherently magical or supernatural. Its significance comes from its immense age and size, its distinctive appearance, and the ecosystem that has developed around it. The clearing provides a natural gathering place for both people and Entheon.

The Great Windmill — Greenvale

The Great Windmill is an enormous functioning structure located in the agricultural heart of Greenvale. Rather than being a conventional factory with a windmill attached, the entire facility was designed around the concept of a gigantic wind-powered processing plant. Its machinery processes agricultural products produced throughout the surrounding region. Its enormous scale makes it visible from considerable distances across Greenvale’s relatively open farmland. The structure is both an important piece of regional infrastructure and one of Greenvale’s defining landmarks. A Secondary Trail connects the main transportation network to the facility.

Great Hawthorne Lake — Hawthorne

Great Hawthorne Lake is a vast inland freshwater lake that dominates the geography of Hawthorne. Its shoreline contains a mixture of wetlands, forests, beaches, rocky sections, and smaller waterways. Rivers and streams flow into and away from the lake, creating a diverse range of freshwater habitats. Lakecrest City was established directly alongside the lake, making the waterway an important part of the city’s history, commerce, and everyday life. The lake is large enough to significantly influence regional travel. Trails naturally follow portions of its shoreline or route around its edges rather than treating it as an obstacle that can simply be crossed.

The Cascading River — Northvale

A distinctive rocky river system runs through Northvale, descending through a sequence of natural levels. Rather than forming one enormous waterfall, the river repeatedly drops over rocky shelves, creating a series of smaller waterfalls, pools, rapids, and cascades. The resulting landscape resembles a natural staircase carved into the terrain. The Cascading River is both a notable landmark and an important freshwater habitat for Entheon. Trails in the surrounding area follow the river where practical, while some sections require travellers to move around the steeper drops.

The Highreach Hot Springs — Highreach / Dunridge / Northvale

Near the mountainous meeting point of Highreach, Dunridge, and Northvale lies a geothermal area containing a series of naturally heated springs. The springs remain warm despite the surrounding high-altitude environment, where snow and cold weather are common. Steam rising from the pools can be visible against the surrounding mountains, particularly during colder weather. The area is known throughout Kaleo as a place to rest and recover during mountain travel. Different pools vary in temperature and surrounding terrain, and the area also provides a distinctive habitat for Entheon adapted to the combination of geothermal warmth and mountainous conditions. The hot springs are accessible through a Secondary Trail branching from the regional mountain routes.

Lume Port — Lume

Lume’s major city is built around a naturally sheltered coastal harbor and serves as the island’s primary connection to the rest of Kaleo. The city functions as a major trading and transportation hub, with ships and ferries carrying passengers, goods, and supplies between Lume and established coastal destinations throughout Kaleo. The port is not a separate settlement from the city. Lume Port is the central maritime district and defining function of the island’s major city. Outside the city, much of Lume remains relatively undeveloped, allowing substantial areas of the island to remain natural Entheon habitat.

The Frozen Expanse — Isen

Rather than a single constructed landmark, Isen’s defining feature is the immense frozen landscape itself. Vast stretches of tundra, snowfield, permafrost, frozen waterways, and exposed rocky ground extend across the region. During periods of particularly severe weather, visibility can become extremely limited and familiar terrain can become difficult to navigate. The Frozen Expanse is one of the least developed environments in Kaleo. Outside Winterhold and its established travel connections, human infrastructure becomes increasingly scarce. The wilderness itself is what defines Isen.

Trails & Transportation

Main Trails

Main Trails are Kaleo’s principal overland travel routes. A Main Trail generally leads toward or through a major city and passes established settlements along the way. Main Trails are not arranged around Gym progression and do not require travellers to visit the Gyms in geographical order. Trails naturally curve around terrain, waterways, mountains, and other geographical features rather than following perfectly straight lines.

Secondary Trails

Secondary Trails branch away from Main Trails when a destination is not directly along the primary route. They may lead to:

  • smaller settlements
  • landmarks
  • alternative destinations
  • areas of particular natural or Entheon interest

A Secondary Trail does not necessarily lead toward another major city or Gym.

Ferry Routes

Established ferry routes provide maritime transportation, particularly around Lume. Ferries connect existing coastal settlements and cities with Lume, allowing travellers and goods to cross the Central Sea without requiring long overland journeys around it. Ferry routes are separate from the Trail network and are represented geographically as maritime routes rather than roads.

Geographic Principle

Kaleo should feel like a coherent inhabited continent rather than a linear adventure path. Cities, settlements, landmarks, wilderness, Trails, and ferry connections exist because of the geography and needs of the people living there. Gym progression is layered onto this world rather than determining its structure.

Wild Entheon Ecology

Wild Entheon populations vary significantly between regions. Not every Entheon species occurs throughout Kaleo; many have specific natural ranges determined by habitat, climate and environmental conditions. Regional encounter frequency is described using three categories:

  • Common — regularly encountered within suitable habitat.
  • Uncommon — naturally present but encountered less frequently.
  • Rare — unusual encounters even within suitable habitat.

An Entheon being classified as Common, Uncommon or Rare describes its wild encounter frequency, not necessarily its overall population, commercial availability or rarity as a species.

Evolutionary Stages

Wild Entheon can exceptionally be encountered in evolved forms, but these are considerably less common than their earlier stages. Stage 3 Entheon are never encountered as normal wild Entheon. Stage 3 forms represent a level of development reached through training and progression rather than a form encountered naturally in the wild. Entheon whose evolution requires a specific evolutionary item also do not naturally appear in their evolved forms. The required item makes deliberate intervention necessary for that evolution to occur.

Veyli

Veyli is an unusual case. Although classified as a Common Entheon species, naturally occurring Veyli populations are restricted to only a small number of regions. Veyli naturally occur in Westmere and Eastmere, where they are nevertheless Rare encounters. Veyli are adaptable but naturally maintain small populations, meaning that encountering one in the wild is considered fortunate. Veyli encountered elsewhere are likely to be owned, bred, traded or transported individuals rather than members of a natural wild population. Veyli’s evolved forms are never wild encounters, as their evolution requires a specific evolutionary item.

Alphan Entheon

An Alphan Entheon is an unusually large and exceptionally developed individual of its species. Alphan Entheon are not a separate species or evolutionary form. They retain the same basic appearance and characteristics as ordinary members of their species but are noticeably larger and generally more physically imposing. Alpha individuals are usually found toward the upper end of the natural level range for their area and are considerably less common than ordinary encounters. An Alphan Entheon is not inherently hostile, although its size, territorial behavior or confidence may make encounters more dangerous. Alpha status does not override normal evolutionary rules:

  • A Stage 3 Entheon cannot appear as a wild Alpha.
  • An item-dependent evolution cannot appear as a wild Alpha.
  • Alpha status does not create a separate evolutionary line.
  • An Alpha remains an individual of its normal species.

The presence of an Alpha can sometimes be inferred from the behavior of other Entheon in the area, particularly when ordinary individuals avoid or give unusual space to a particular territory.

Wild Entheon Distribution

Wild Entheon are distributed according to the ecology, terrain, climate and level range of each region. Encounter frequency is divided into four categories:

  • Common: Regularly encountered within suitable habitats.
  • Uncommon: Present in the region but encountered less frequently.
  • Rare: Unusual encounters that may require exploration or suitable conditions.
  • Rare Evolution: Evolved Entheon that can naturally occur in the wild despite their rarity.

Some species have additional encounter conditions, such as time of day or geographic exclusivity. Stage 3 Entheon do not occur as wild encounters. Certain special-evolution forms also do not occur naturally in the wild, and Veyli’s evolved forms require specific catalysts and are therefore excluded from wild encounters.

Gym Leaders

Gym Leaders are prominent Trainers who serve as the public representatives of their respective Gyms. Each specializes in a particular Affinity, but their personal identity, profession, battle philosophy and approach to training are not necessarily defined by that Affinity alone. A Gym Leader’s established roster consists of six Entheon. During Gym challenges, Leaders may use an appropriate subset of that roster depending on the challenger’s progression.

Everhope Gym — Rowan Vale

Region: Westmere Affinity: Gale Age: 32

Appearance

Rowan has a lean, athletic build and an easygoing appearance that makes him look more like an experienced outdoorsman than a formal Gym Leader. He has medium-length dark-blond hair that falls loosely around his face and neck, deliberately kept long enough for him to feel the breeze moving through it. He has light grey-blue eyes and a perpetual hint of stubble. His clothing combines practical outdoor gear with a slightly stylish edge: a lightweight weatherproof jacket, fitted shirt, durable trousers and well-worn hiking boots. His jacket is usually left open, even when the weather would suggest otherwise. Rowan likes the feeling of wind in his hair and rarely bothers tying it back unless he genuinely needs to. If the wind is blowing, he is perfectly happy to let it do its thing.

Personality

Rowan is relaxed, observant and quietly confident. He rarely raises his voice and does not particularly care about maintaining the intimidating image some Gym Leaders cultivate. He enjoys talking to challengers and will often have a casual conversation with them before the battle. He is, however, extremely observant. Rowan notices small things: how a Trainer interacts with their Entheon, whether they panic when something goes wrong, and whether they adapt their plans or stubbornly continue with them. He believes that a good Trainer should not need everything to go according to plan.

Background

Rowan grew up around Westmere’s forests and developed a fascination with climbing, hiking and weather patterns. Before becoming a Gym Leader, he worked as an outdoor guide, taking people through some of Westmere’s more difficult wilderness routes. His experience with Gale Entheon began through those expeditions. Rather than seeking out powerful Entheon specifically, he developed relationships with species that naturally accompanied him through the region. Eventually, his reputation as an exceptionally adaptable Trainer led to him being offered the position at Everhope Gym.

Gym Philosophy

“You can’t control the wind. You can only learn how to move with it.”

Rowan sees battling as an exercise in adaptation. A Trainer can enter a battle with the perfect strategy, but the opponent gets to make decisions too. Their Entheon may behave unexpectedly, the environment may change, or a carefully planned attack may simply fail. The important question is not “What was your plan?” It is: “What do you do when your plan stops working?”

Battle Style

Rowan’s battles are fast, mobile and reactive. He prefers Entheon that can reposition themselves, exploit openings and force opponents to continually reconsider their approach. He does not necessarily overwhelm opponents with raw power. Instead, he creates situations where an opponent’s positioning, timing or assumptions become liabilities. His battles tend to feel dynamic rather than predictable.

Gym Assessment

The Everhope Gym does not simply test whether a challenger can defeat Rowan. The Gym’s assessment is built around adaptability and communication. Before the battle, challengers must navigate a wind-based obstacle course with their Entheon. The exact route changes depending on environmental conditions, and some obstacles cannot be overcome simply through strength. Rowan watches how the Trainer communicates with their Entheon:

  • Do they adapt when the original route fails?
  • Do they understand what their Entheon is comfortable doing?
  • Do they give useful commands rather than simply shouting instructions?
  • Do they recognize when their Entheon needs to change approach?
  • Can they remain composed when something unexpected happens?

The final battle then tests the same principle under pressure. A challenger who defeats Rowan through brute force but demonstrates poor communication may receive considerably more criticism than someone who loses narrowly but demonstrates exceptional adaptability.

Established Entheon Roster

Rowan’s established six-Entheon roster consists of six distinct evolutionary lines, with no duplicate evolution families.

  1. Nymbril — Wild/Gale Rowan’s signature partner and the clearest representation of his philosophy. Nymbril’s mobility and ability to exploit its surroundings make it particularly suited to Rowan’s approach to battle.
  2. Rookane — Metal/Gale Rowan’s dedicated aerial specialist. Its combination of aerial control and Metal affinity gives him a different tactical approach from his other mobile Entheon.
  3. Avenn — Gale A highly maneuverable specialist suited to precision attacks, rapid directional changes and controlling the pace of an encounter.
  4. Sovel — Gale A playful and unpredictable member of the team that excels at sudden changes of direction and momentum.
  5. Caelune — Gale/Tide A highly unusual aerial Entheon whose biological jetpack allows it to launch itself rapidly through the battlefield and make sharp turns. Its secondary Tide Affinity gives Rowan additional tactical options.
  6. Quiblet — Flame Rowan’s wildcard. Although it does not possess the Gale Affinity, Quiblet is airborne and fits his philosophy of movement and adaptability. Its inclusion also prevents his team from becoming overly predictable.

Entheon Levels

Rowan’s established Entheon range from approximately Lv. 10–16. The exact levels and composition used during a Gym challenge depend on the challenger’s progression, allowing Rowan to provide an appropriate assessment without necessarily revealing or using the full strength of his established team. His full six-Entheon roster represents Rowan the Trainer; the smaller Gym battle roster represents Rowan as an assessor.

Gullhaven Gym — Maya Corwin

Region: Seawick Affinity: Tide Age: 29

Appearance

Maya has the athletic physique you’d expect from someone who has spent years surfing, swimming and working as a lifeguard. She has sun-kissed skin, bright blue-green eyes and long dark-auburn hair, usually worn in a loose braid or ponytail while she is working. Away from work, she is just as likely to leave it loose. Her clothing is practical rather than ceremonial. She typically wears a sleeveless rash guard, lightweight board shorts, water-resistant sandals and a weathered rescue jacket that can be thrown on when needed. A whistle hangs around her neck almost constantly, largely because she has worn one for so long that she barely notices it anymore. Her battered surfboard is rarely far away, covered in stickers and the accumulated marks of years spent in the water.

Personality

Maya is energetic, outgoing and naturally approachable. She is competitive in a cheerful way and might challenge someone she has just met to a race along the beach without giving it a second thought. That easygoing attitude changes immediately when someone’s safety is involved. Her experience as a lifeguard has given her a strong protective instinct, and she takes reckless behaviour seriously. Maya doesn’t equate courage with ignoring danger. She respects people who understand their own limitations and recognize when circumstances have changed. Losing a battle doesn’t particularly bother her; failing to learn from one does.

Background

Maya was raised in Gullhaven and grew up around the sea. Swimming became second nature to her at an early age, and she discovered surfing during her early teens. She eventually trained as a lifeguard and spent years working along Gullhaven’s beaches, becoming one of the area’s most experienced rescue swimmers. That career also brought her into frequent contact with Tide Entheon. Many of her strongest relationships with Entheon developed naturally through beach patrols, rescues and countless hours spent in and around the water rather than through deliberately seeking out powerful battle partners. Her reputation as both an accomplished Trainer and an experienced lifeguard eventually resulted in her appointment as Gullhaven’s Gym Leader.

Gym Philosophy

“The water doesn’t care how strong you are. Learn to read it.”

For Maya, Tide represents flow, adaptation and awareness. She doesn’t believe the strongest Trainer is necessarily the one capable of producing the most powerful attack. A capable Trainer understands what is happening around them, recognizes when circumstances shift and knows when to advance, change direction or give ground. Maya often compares battling to surfing. A surfer doesn’t defeat a wave through brute force. They read its movement, work with it and recognize the precise moment when they can turn that movement to their advantage.

Battle Style

Maya’s battles revolve around momentum and constant adjustment. Rather than committing herself to either offense or defense, she moves fluidly between the two. Her Entheon may retreat deliberately, reposition themselves and then suddenly turn that apparent loss of ground into an offensive opportunity. She likes creating situations in which the opponent has to make difficult choices: whether to attack immediately or wait, whether to pursue or reposition, and whether to persist with an existing strategy or abandon it. As a result, her battles tend to develop a distinctive rhythm, alternating between periods of pressure and sudden changes in tempo.

Gym Assessment

The Gullhaven Gym incorporates the surrounding water into its assessment. Challengers are required to work alongside their Entheon through a series of aquatic exercises, including navigating changing currents, reaching designated locations and responding to simulated rescue situations. Physical strength alone isn’t what Maya is evaluating. She pays particular attention to whether the Trainer understands their Entheon’s abilities and limitations, responds effectively when conditions change, recognizes when an approach has become unsafe and communicates without trying to micromanage every action. Most importantly, she looks at whether a Trainer will prioritize their Entheon’s wellbeing over simply completing the challenge. A challenger who recklessly forces an Entheon through a dangerous situation can fail the assessment even if they technically complete the obstacle. To Maya, recognizing when to stop is evidence of good judgment rather than weakness.

Established Entheon Roster

Maya’s established team contains six distinct evolutionary lines, with no repeated evolution families.

  1. Vessa — Tide Maya’s original partner and the Entheon that accompanied her during the early years when she was learning to surf.
  2. Koroa — Tide A powerful swimmer capable of manipulating the battlefield through water and controlling the space around an opponent.
  3. Selka — Tide A quick and agile aquatic Entheon that gives Maya an option focused on rapid repositioning.
  4. Veysha — Tide A less conventional member of Maya’s team, providing a tactical option that opponents may not immediately anticipate.
  5. Mirel — Tide A versatile member capable of helping Maya transition between defensive and offensive approaches while controlling the battle’s tempo.
  6. Pirello — Tide (Ace) Maya’s strongest and most trusted battle partner. Pirello embodies her approach to battling: understand the current situation, wait for the right opening, and then use the momentum of the battle to make a decisive push.

Entheon Levels

Maya’s established Entheon range from approximately Lv. 14–20. The levels and specific members she uses in an official Gym challenge can vary according to the challenger’s progression. This allows her to provide an appropriate assessment without necessarily revealing or deploying the full strength of her established team. Her complete roster represents Maya as an experienced Trainer, while the smaller team used during a Gym challenge represents Maya in her role as an assessor.

Thermalis Gym — Darius Voss

Region: Highreach Affinity: Fire/Stone Age: 41

Appearance

Darius is broad-shouldered and powerfully built, with the physique of someone who has spent decades doing physical work rather than training for appearance. He has dark, slightly greying hair, a short beard and deep brown eyes. He usually wears a heavy work apron over a simple sleeveless shirt, reinforced work trousers and sturdy boots. His forearms show the occasional small scar or burn from years around furnaces and stonework. Protective gloves are usually tucked into his belt rather than worn unless he is actually working. His clothing is practical and well-used, but he takes pride in keeping his tools in excellent condition.

Personality

Darius is calm, patient and straightforward. He isn’t particularly interested in impressing people and has little tolerance for unnecessary theatrics. He can initially come across as intimidating because of his size and reserved manner, but he is actually remarkably approachable. He enjoys teaching people how things work and has a habit of giving practical advice even when nobody asked for it. He believes quality comes from repetition, patience and accepting that mistakes are part of learning. He is also stubborn. Once Darius believes something is worth doing, convincing him otherwise can be extremely difficult.

Background

Darius grew up in Thermalis and comes from a family of craftspeople who have worked with the region’s volcanic stone and mineral resources for generations. He initially became a blacksmith, eventually expanding into stonework and specialized equipment used by Trainers and Entheon researchers. His work brought him into close contact with Fire and Stone Entheon, and he gradually developed a reputation as an exceptionally capable Trainer. Rather than abandoning his trade when he became Gym Leader, Darius incorporated the Gym into his workshop. The Thermalis Gym therefore doubles as a functioning forge and stoneworking facility.

Gym Philosophy

“Heat reveals what something is made of. Pressure reveals what it can become.”

Darius believes that strength isn’t something you simply possess. It is something you build. Fire represents transformation: applying enough energy to change something. Stone represents endurance: remaining stable when that energy and pressure are applied. His philosophy extends to Trainers as much as Entheon. He doesn’t expect challengers to be perfect. He wants to see whether they can withstand setbacks, learn from mistakes and continue improving rather than falling apart when their first plan fails.

Battle Style

Darius favours controlled pressure and endurance. His battles tend to begin deliberately rather than explosively. He establishes his position, forces the opponent to commit and gradually increases the pressure. His Entheon combine defensive resilience with powerful bursts of Fire or Stone attacks. Rather than constantly switching tactics, Darius is comfortable allowing a battle to become a contest of endurance—provided he has prepared his Entheon properly for it. He is particularly good at punishing opponents who become impatient.

Gym Assessment

The Thermalis Gym assessment takes place throughout Darius’s working forge. Challengers must complete a series of practical tasks alongside their Entheon, involving heat, pressure and stone. These aren’t simply strength tests. Some tasks require careful control, others require persistence, and some require recognizing when continuing to push would be counterproductive. Darius observes:

  • Can the Trainer remain calm when something goes wrong?
  • Do they understand the difference between persistence and recklessness?
  • Can they adjust their approach without abandoning the objective?
  • Do they trust their Entheon to perform tasks independently?
  • Can they work patiently toward a result rather than rushing for immediate success?

The final battle tests the same qualities. Darius wants to see whether the challenger can take pressure without losing their composure.

Established Entheon Roster

Darius’s established team consists of six distinct evolutionary lines, with no repeated evolution families.

  1. Dovik — Stone A dependable Stone Entheon that represents the endurance and stability at the heart of Darius’s philosophy.
  2. Arko — Flame Darius’s straightforward offensive specialist, providing a sharp contrast to his more defensive Entheon.
  3. Braska — Fire/Stone The first member of his roster to combine both of his specialized Affinities, representing the balance between transformation and endurance.
  4. Brindrel — Verdant/Stone A less obvious member of Darius’s team whose secondary Stone Affinity makes it a natural fit despite its Verdant aspect. It gives him an option that opponents may not immediately expect from a Fire/Stone Gym.
  5. Tarnelle — Stone/Metal Darius’s defensive specialist. Its Metal Affinity complements its Stone characteristics and reinforces his emphasis on resilience and sustained pressure.
  6. Quivane — Flame/Volt (Ace) Darius’s most trusted battle partner and his greatest tactical surprise. Its Volt Affinity gives his otherwise methodical team an additional dimension, allowing him to punish challengers who assume they have completely prepared for a Fire/Stone specialist.

Entheon Levels

Darius’s established Entheon range from approximately Lv. 20–26. The levels and specific members he uses in an official Gym challenge can vary according to the challenger’s progression. This allows him to provide an appropriate assessment without necessarily revealing or deploying the full strength of his established team. His complete roster represents Darius as an experienced Trainer, while the smaller team used during a Gym challenge represents Darius in his role as an assessor.

Harveston Gym — Elara Greenfield

Region: Westmere Affinity: Verdant Age: 36

Appearance

Elara has a sturdy, healthy build from years of agricultural work, with warm brown skin, green eyes and thick chestnut hair that she usually wears in a loose braid. Her clothing is practical and comfortable: a linen shirt with rolled-up sleeves, durable trousers, sturdy work boots and a weathered gardening apron. Small traces of her work are almost always visible—soil beneath her fingernails, a few leaves caught in her hair, or a smudge of dirt on one sleeve. She doesn’t particularly care. Rather than wearing anything resembling a traditional Gym Leader uniform, Elara dresses much like the farmers and horticulturalists she works alongside.

Personality

Elara is warm, patient and quietly stubborn. She has a nurturing personality and tends to treat people much like she treats plants: give them the right conditions, give them room to grow, and don’t expect them to develop at exactly the same pace. She’s friendly and approachable, but her patience has limits. She has little sympathy for people who mistreat Entheon, plants or the land around them. She is also surprisingly competitive once a battle begins. Her gentle demeanour shouldn’t be mistaken for passivity.

Background

Elara grew up around Harveston’s agricultural communities and became involved in farming from an early age. Her family maintained a mixed farm, growing crops and raising produce suited to the region’s fertile land. As she grew older, she became particularly interested in the relationship between agriculture and Verdant Entheon. She eventually began working with local farmers to understand how Entheon could help maintain crops, manage difficult terrain and protect farmland from wild creatures. Her success eventually led to her becoming Harveston’s Gym Leader. She still maintains her own agricultural plots, and the Gym incorporates working gardens and cultivated land rather than being separated from the community around it.

Gym Philosophy

“You can’t force something to grow. You can only give it what it needs.”

Elara believes that good Trainers understand growth, patience and individual potential. Every Entheon develops differently. Some thrive under pressure. Others need time and encouragement. Some are naturally aggressive, while others are better suited to support or defensive roles. A Trainer who tries to force every Entheon into the same mould will eventually limit their own team.

Battle Style

Elara prefers sustainable pressure and battlefield control. Rather than immediately trying to overwhelm an opponent, she establishes favourable conditions and gradually makes the battlefield work against them. Her Entheon use terrain, positioning and sustained effects to restrict an opponent’s options while maintaining their own resilience. She is particularly fond of battles where her opponent becomes increasingly frustrated because their original strategy gradually stops working.

Gym Assessment

The Harveston Gym assessment takes place among cultivated gardens, fields and greenhouses. Challengers must work with their Entheon to complete a series of tasks involving cultivation, navigation and maintaining living environments. Some challenges require physical strength, while others reward patience, observation and careful cooperation. Elara watches for:

  • Does the Trainer understand what their Entheon actually needs?
  • Can they adapt their approach to different individuals?
  • Are they willing to let their Entheon contribute rather than controlling every action?
  • Can they recognize gradual progress rather than expecting immediate results?
  • Do they demonstrate care for the environment around them?

The final battle tests whether the challenger can maintain a coherent strategy over time rather than simply attempting to end the battle as quickly as possible.

Established Entheon Roster

Elara’s established team consists of six distinct evolutionary lines, with no repeated evolution families.

  1. Meliu — Verdant A gentle and plant-associated Entheon that reflects Elara’s nurturing approach and her connection to horticulture.
  2. Orven — Verdant A distinct single-stage Verdant Entheon that gives Elara another established species without relying on an evolutionary line already represented on her team.
  3. Mirel — Tide/Verdant A versatile member that brings a secondary Affinity into Elara’s team while maintaining a strong connection to cultivated and natural environments.
  4. Rilsa — Verdant/Tide A natural fit for Harveston’s fertile environment and a member that gives Elara additional options through its Tide Affinity.
  5. Brindrel — Verdant/Stone The more physically imposing member of Elara’s team. Its Stone Affinity provides additional resilience and allows her to shift away from purely defensive Verdant strategies.
  6. Virenne — Verdant/Gale (Ace) Elara’s fastest and most mobile team member. Virenne gives her battle style an important change of pace and prevents opponents from assuming that her team will simply rely on slow, sustained pressure.

Entheon Levels

This allows her to provide an appropriate assessment without necessarily revealing or deploying the full strength of her established team. Her complete roster represents Elara as an experienced Trainer, while the smaller team used during a Gym challenge represents Elara in her role as an assessor.

Lakecrest Gym — Lena Hartwell

Region: Westmere Affinity: Volt Age: 31

Appearance

Lena has a lean, athletic build and an energetic presence. She has dark, almost black hair worn around shoulder length, with several vivid red-dyed streaks running through the lower layers. The red is deliberately a little uneven rather than perfectly salon-styled, giving her a casual rocker’s aesthetic. Her eyes are bright hazel, and she usually wears a mischievous expression that makes it difficult to tell whether she is about to explain something technical or make a joke. Her clothing blends practical electrical workwear with a distinctly modern rock-inspired style: a fitted black utility jacket, dark work trousers, sturdy boots and a tool belt containing various small instruments. She sometimes wears a band-style shirt underneath her jacket, and insulated gloves are usually shoved into one of her pockets rather than actually worn. Small electrical motifs appear on some of her equipment, but she avoids dressing like a literal walking lightning bolt.

Personality

Lena is energetic, curious and extremely quick-thinking. She loves figuring out how things work and has a tendency to start explaining an interesting technical problem before realizing the person she is talking to may have absolutely no idea what she is talking about. She is playful and enjoys teasing challengers, particularly when they become too predictable. Lena believes almost every problem can be solved by understanding the system. However, she also knows that understanding a pattern is not enough. Once an opponent has figured out what she is doing, she expects herself and her Entheon to be capable of changing the system before it can be exploited. Her enthusiasm occasionally leads her to overcomplicate things that could have been solved much more simply.

Background

Lena grew up around Lakecrest and became fascinated with electrical systems at a young age. She studied electrical engineering and eventually returned to the region, where she began working on power infrastructure and small-scale technology projects. Her work brought her into contact with Volt Entheon, particularly those living around the lake’s infrastructure and nearby settlements. She eventually began designing equipment that allowed communities to make better use of naturally occurring electrical energy while also developing as a Trainer. Outside of her engineering work, Lena has a strong interest in rock music and occasionally performs with a local band. When the Lakecrest Gym needed a new Leader, Lena was an obvious candidate. The Gym itself doubles as a demonstration workshop, with generators, electrical systems and experimental equipment incorporated into the challenge.

Gym Philosophy

“Understand the system. Then break the pattern.”

Lena believes that battles are problems to be solved. She encourages Trainers to observe what is happening, identify patterns and recognize what their opponent is trying to accomplish. But she also believes that understanding a pattern isn’t enough. Once an opponent has figured out what you are doing, you need to be capable of changing the system before they exploit it.

Battle Style

Lena’s battles focus on speed, disruption and exploiting predictable behaviour. She likes forcing opponents to react rather than allowing them to establish their preferred rhythm. Her Entheon may rapidly change targets, reposition or use Volt techniques to create openings for another member. She particularly enjoys punishing Trainers who rely too heavily on a single strategy. Her battles can feel chaotic to inexperienced opponents, but there is usually a very deliberate structure underneath the apparent chaos.

Gym Assessment

The Lakecrest Gym is effectively a giant electrical puzzle. Challengers must navigate a series of interconnected rooms where switches, circuits and mechanical systems affect one another. Activating one system may disable another, open a route elsewhere or change the environment in a different section. The challenge isn’t designed to test technical knowledge. Everything necessary can be learned through observation. Lena watches for:

  • Does the Trainer observe before acting?
  • Can they recognize patterns?
  • Do they learn from previous mistakes?
  • Can they adapt when their assumptions are wrong?
  • Do they communicate effectively with their Entheon?
  • Can they distinguish between a complicated solution and a genuinely useful one?

The final battle tests the same principle. Lena deliberately establishes patterns during the battle and then breaks them, forcing the challenger to recognize that their opponent has adapted.

Established Entheon Roster

Lena’s established team consists of six distinct evolutionary lines, with no repeated evolution families.

  1. Tivvi — Volt A small, hyperactive electric Entheon that perfectly matches Lena’s energetic personality. Tivvi is constantly attracted to electrical equipment and can often be found investigating the Gym’s machinery.
  2. Keln — Metal A practical fit for Lena’s engineering background. Keln gives her a strong connection to machinery and the Metal Affinity while providing a different combat identity from her Volt Entheon.
  3. Avenn — Gale Lena’s fast aerial specialist. Its exceptional maneuverability gives her a way to control positioning and introduces another dimension to her battle style.
  4. Rookane — Metal/Gale A more substantial aerial threat that combines mobility with Metal resilience. Rookane allows Lena to maintain pressure from above while continuing to challenge an opponent’s assumptions about her team.
  5. Veyro — Gale/Volt A highly mobile Volt-affiliated aerial Entheon and a natural expression of Lena’s personality. Veyro’s affinity for strong winds and thunderstorms makes it particularly well suited to Lena’s energetic, unconventional style.
  6. Stage 2 Dragon-line Entheon — Dragon (Ace) Lena’s hidden ace and the member she reserves for exceptional challengers.

Ace Selection

Lena does not normally use her Dragon-line Entheon during standard Gym challenges. She reserves it for exceptional Trainers—particularly challengers who arrive with a reputation for being unusually strong, or those whose confidence crosses into outright arrogance. Sometimes Lena will have heard stories about a challenger beforehand. Other times, she recognizes it during the assessment: a challenger who dismisses her earlier Entheon, assumes they have figured out her strategy, or begins treating victory as inevitable. That is when Lena brings out her ace. The Dragon-line Entheon represents the ultimate expression of her Gym philosophy. Lena deliberately allows opponents to identify patterns in her battle style, only to completely break those patterns with an Entheon they were not expecting. People who have witnessed Lena use her Dragon-line ace often talk about the experience afterward, contributing to its growing reputation among Trainers.

Entheon Levels

Lena’s established Entheon range from approximately Lv. 28–34. The levels and specific members she uses in an official Gym challenge can vary according to the challenger’s progression. Her Dragon-line Stage 2 Entheon is generally reserved for exceptional challengers rather than ordinary Gym battles. Her complete roster represents Lena as an experienced Trainer, while the smaller team used during a Gym challenge represents Lena in her role as an assessor.

Northreach Gym — Magnus Veyr

Region: Highreach Affinity: Metal/Frost Age: 44

Appearance

Magnus is tall, broad-shouldered and heavily built, with a weathered appearance that reflects years spent working in the northern climate. He has pale skin, steel-grey eyes and thick dark-blond hair beginning to grey at the temples. His beard is kept short and neatly trimmed. He dresses for practicality rather than style: a heavy insulated coat, reinforced trousers, sturdy winter boots and thick gloves. Beneath the outer layers he usually wears a simple dark shirt and a reinforced utility harness. His clothing contains subtle Metal detailing, including reinforced buckles, clasps and protective plates. He carries himself with an almost military-like posture, though he has never actually served in the military.

Personality

Magnus is quiet, disciplined and exceptionally patient. He rarely raises his voice and dislikes unnecessary theatrics. He listens carefully before speaking and has a habit of considering a problem from several angles before committing to a decision. Despite his imposing appearance, he is not particularly intimidating once someone gets to know him. He has a dry sense of humour that tends to appear unexpectedly. Magnus respects preparation above almost everything else. He doesn’t believe that courage means charging into danger. To him, courage is knowing what you’re facing and being prepared to endure it anyway.

Background

Magnus grew up in Northreach and spent much of his early life working in the region’s mining and metalworking industries. The harsh environment taught him the importance of preparation, equipment and endurance. A poorly maintained tool could become a serious problem when working far from civilization, and a failure to prepare for sudden changes in weather could be dangerous. His relationship with Metal and Frost Entheon developed naturally through this lifestyle. He eventually became a specialist in cold-weather equipment and reinforced tools used by explorers, miners and Trainers travelling through Highreach. His reputation as a Trainer eventually led to him becoming Northreach’s Gym Leader.

Gym Philosophy

“Steel is forged by what it survives. Frost hardens those it faces.”

Magnus believes that strength is not demonstrated by avoiding adversity, but by enduring it and coming out stronger. Metal represents discipline, structure and the ability to withstand pressure. Frost represents hardship: something that cannot simply be ignored or overcome through brute force. His Gym therefore tests whether a Trainer can keep functioning when things become difficult. He doesn’t particularly care whether a challenger has the perfect strategy from the beginning. He cares whether they can adapt, endure setbacks and keep making good decisions under pressure.

Battle Style

Magnus favours attrition, defensive positioning and controlled counterattacks. His battles rarely begin explosively. He establishes a defensive position, watches how the opponent attacks and gradually forces them to expend resources. His Metal Entheon provide durability and defensive pressure, while Frost Entheon create opportunities to slow, restrict or disrupt opponents. Magnus is particularly fond of turning an opponent’s aggression against them. If a challenger repeatedly throws themselves at his defenses without adapting, he will happily let them exhaust themselves.

Gym Assessment

Northreach Gym is designed around a simulated northern expedition. Challengers must navigate a cold environment while managing limited resources, changing paths and mechanical obstacles. Some sections require their Entheon to assist directly, while others test the Trainer’s ability to recognize when their current approach is becoming inefficient. Magnus observes:

  • Does the Trainer prepare before acting?
  • Can they remain calm when conditions change?
  • Do they understand their Entheon’s strengths and limitations?
  • Can they conserve resources rather than constantly attacking?
  • Do they know when to retreat and reassess?
  • Can they remain effective when their original strategy fails?

The final battle is deliberately longer and more methodical than the earlier Gym battles. Magnus isn’t looking for the Trainer who can produce the biggest attack. He’s looking for the Trainer who can still think clearly after everything has gone wrong.

Established Entheon Roster

Magnus’s established team consists of six distinct evolutionary lines, with no repeated evolution families.

  1. Keln — Metal A sturdy Metal Entheon and Magnus’s dependable opener. Keln represents the simple reliability that Magnus values in both tools and partners.
  2. Talvi — Frost A surprisingly fast Frost specialist. Its playful appearance contrasts with the more serious members of Magnus’s team, making it an effective way to disrupt an opponent’s expectations.
  3. Pellune — Frost/Mystic A more technical Frost fighter capable of combining its cold-based abilities with Mystic techniques. This gives Magnus a tactical option that doesn’t rely purely on physical endurance.
  4. Rookane — Metal/Gale Magnus’s mobile Metal specialist. Its Gale Affinity gives the otherwise methodical team a much-needed increase in mobility and allows it to attack from unexpected angles.
  5. Fennovar — Frost/Stone A heavily built Frost Entheon whose Stone characteristics make it exceptionally durable. Fennovar represents the literal combination of cold and physical resilience that defines Magnus’s Gym.
  6. Ruun — Frost/Umbral (Ace) Magnus’s rarest and most formidable partner. Ruun is a solitary, territorial dark yeti that normally avoids settlements and has an almost folkloric reputation among people living near its habitat. Its Frost abilities combine with Umbral energy to allow it to disappear into snowstorms and darkness.

Entheon Levels

Magnus’s established Entheon range from approximately Lv. 34–40. His earlier team members are used for ordinary Gym assessments, while Ruun is reserved for particularly capable challengers and represents the upper end of Magnus’s established strength. Ruun’s inclusion is not because Magnus simply happened to capture a powerful rare Entheon. The two have a long-standing relationship built on mutual respect, and Magnus is one of the few people Ruun willingly fights alongside. Many Trainers know Magnus has a “snow beast” somewhere in his team, but relatively few have actually seen him use it.

Fairhaven Gym — Celeste Laurent

Region: Eastmere Affinity: Mystic Age: 38

Appearance

Celeste is an elegant woman with a graceful, almost effortlessly poised presence. She has long, dark auburn hair that falls well past her shoulders, usually worn loose or gathered into an elegant half-up style. Her eyes are a striking violet-grey, and she has a refined complexion and an athletic but distinctly feminine build. Her clothing resembles sophisticated evening wear adapted for movement rather than a traditional Trainer’s outfit: a fitted dark dress with layered fabric panels, elegant boots and a tailored jacket that she removes before serious battles. Subtle silver jewellery and violet gemstones complete the look. Nothing about her appearance is excessive; everything is carefully chosen. There is something inherently captivating about Celeste. She knows exactly how to use eye contact, posture and a perfectly timed smile, and she is quite comfortable allowing someone else to wonder whether she is flirting with them or simply enjoying watching them become distracted.

Personality

Celeste is sophisticated, confident and extraordinarily perceptive. She is charming without being overly familiar, flirtatious without being crude and enjoys making people slightly unsure of themselves. She has an excellent sense of humour and genuinely enjoys intelligent conversation. She is particularly fond of people who can surprise her. Celeste rarely reveals everything she is thinking. Even when she is being completely honest, she has a habit of saying things in a way that leaves people wondering whether there was another meaning hidden underneath. She is not cruel or manipulative by nature. Her fascination with psychology comes from genuine curiosity about people. She also has a surprisingly warm side that she shows primarily toward people she trusts and her Entheon.

Background

Celeste grew up in Fairhaven’s upper social circles but deliberately chose not to pursue the conventional career expected of her. She became fascinated with Mystic Entheon and eventually studied perception, cognition and the ways humans and Entheon interpret their surroundings. Over time, she developed into an accomplished Trainer whose battles became known for their psychological complexity. Her reputation eventually brought her to the Fairhaven Gym. Rather than turning the Gym into a conventional battle facility, Celeste transformed it into an elegant series of salons, mirrored chambers and carefully constructed environments where challengers are forced to question what they are seeing.

Gym Philosophy

“The moment you believe you understand what you see, you’ve already given me an advantage.”

Celeste believes that perception is one of the most powerful weapons a Trainer possesses. A battle isn’t simply about knowing what an Entheon can do. It is about understanding what your opponent wants you to believe they are doing. She teaches that confidence is useful, but certainty is dangerous.

Battle Style

Celeste specializes in misdirection, prediction and psychological pressure. She deliberately creates uncertainty about her next move, using her Entheon’s different abilities and secondary Affinities to make opponents question their assumptions. Rather than overwhelming opponents immediately, she encourages them to commit to an interpretation of the battle and then punishes them for it. Her Dragons provide an important contrast: when Celeste decides that subtlety has served its purpose, she can suddenly bring overwhelming physical power onto the field.

Gym Assessment

The Fairhaven Gym is less a physical obstacle course and more a test of perception and judgment. Challengers move through a sequence of elegant rooms containing mirrors, illusions, misleading pathways and situations in which the obvious solution isn’t necessarily the correct one. Celeste observes:

  • Can the Trainer distinguish observation from assumption?
  • Do they notice inconsistencies?
  • Can they remain composed when they aren’t certain what is happening?
  • Do they understand their Entheon’s individual behaviour rather than relying solely on species expectations?
  • Can they recognize when an opponent is deliberately trying to manipulate their decisions?
  • Most importantly, can they change their interpretation when new information appears?

The final battle is designed around the same principle. Celeste wants the challenger to understand that being fooled once is not failure; refusing to reconsider after discovering you were fooled is.

Established Entheon Roster

Celeste’s established team consists of six distinct evolutionary lines, with no repeated evolution families.

  1. Veysha — Mystic/Umbral An unsettling Mystic Entheon whose Umbral Affinity complements Celeste’s emphasis on misdirection and perception. Veysha allows her to introduce uncertainty from the very beginning of a battle.
  2. Thessane — Mystic/Frost An elegant Mystic/Frost Entheon that combines refined Mystic techniques with Frost-based control. Thessane provides Celeste with a more methodical option when subtle disruption isn’t enough.
  3. Elvarin — Mystic/Radiant The shrine-maiden-like member of Celeste’s team. Elvarin’s serene presence and spiritual nature make it an especially natural fit for Celeste’s sophisticated aesthetic and Mystic specialization.
  4. Lumaryn — Radiant/Mystic A powerful Stage 2 Entheon combining Radiant and Mystic Affinities. Lumaryn gives Celeste another avenue for perception and support while providing a markedly different presence from Elvarin despite their shared secondary Affinity.
  5. Yori — Dragon Celeste’s first Dragon and a deliberate escalation in raw physical power. Yori provides a dramatic contrast to the more controlled and deceptive members of her team.
  6. Uveryn — Mystic/Dragon (Ace) Celeste’s ultimate battle partner. Uveryn combines her signature Mystic Affinity with the overwhelming potential of a Dragon, making it the perfect culmination of her battle philosophy.

Entheon Levels

Celeste’s established Entheon range from approximately Lv. 40–46. Her earlier members allow her to conduct a conventional Gym assessment without immediately revealing the full strength of her roster. The later members provide progressively greater pressure, culminating in Uveryn at the upper end of her established range. Celeste’s Dragons are not simply there to provide overwhelming power. She uses them to punish challengers who become too certain that they have correctly understood her strategy. By the time Uveryn enters the field, Celeste expects the challenger to have learned an important lesson from the Gym: Knowing what you see is not the same as understanding what it means.
