Species Entry Framework

Each species entry is structured around the following core categories:

  1. Species concept — core identity and archetype.
  2. Anatomy & appearance — body plan, proportions, head, limbs, wings/tail and distinctive anatomy.
  3. Surface & coloration — material/texture, primary colours, secondary colours and markings.
  4. Affinity manifestation — how each affinity appears biologically, visually and functionally.
  5. Personality & behaviour — temperament, habits and ecological behaviour.
  6. Combat identity — broad fighting style and characteristic affinity use.
  7. Evolution progression — how each stage develops from the previous one.
  8. Silhouette priority — the most important visual feature for recognition.

Not every species requires every category to be stated separately; descriptions prioritize the information most relevant to that species.

Global design rules

  • These are original species, not renamed copies of existing Pokémon.
  • Existing creatures may inspire broad archetypes or body plans, but each final design needs its own identity.
  • Evolution stages should remain recognizably related while becoming meaningfully more developed.
  • Normal three-stage lines retain their established affinities from stage 2 into stage 3.
  • Branching evolution may intentionally replace the base affinity.
  • Affinity should influence anatomy, behaviour and abilities without reducing every species to an elemental cliché.
  • Common species may be simple; rare and Legendary species may be more elaborate.
  • Legendary status does not automatically mean enormous size.
  • No creature should accidentally duplicate another creature’s primary silhouette or ecological niche.
  • Written descriptions establish the intended canonical species identity, anatomy, behaviour and Affinity expression.

Size & Physical Scale

Each Entheon species has a typical adult physical scale. Individual specimens naturally vary, but the measurements below establish the normal reference point for visual description and narrative comparison.

  • Average Height: Standing height for bipeds; shoulder height for quadrupeds; maximum natural body height for serpentine, floating or otherwise non-standard body plans.
  • Average Length: Front of the body to the end of the body or tail in a natural resting posture. Wings, extended limbs and other temporary projections are excluded.
  • Size Class: A human-relative reference based primarily on the creature's established height and overall physical presence.

For quadrupedal Entheon, shoulder height must not be interpreted as total head height. A large bear-, rhino- or dragon-like creature can therefore have a shoulder height below a human's standing height while still being substantially larger and heavier than the human.

Size Classes

These measurements are intended as species-level averages rather than hard limits.

Umbral / Dragon — Legendary Nethryss is intended to have an exceptionally intimidating presence.

Species concept: skeletal armored dragon

  • Massive quadrupedal dragon.
  • Extremely broad, muscular body.

  • Large clawed limbs.

  • Rib-like structures protect its chest and abdomen.
  • Vertebrae-like armor plates continue along its spine.
  • Jaw has exposed, fang-like bone structures.
  • Long black claws.
  • Deep crimson or violet eyes burning from beneath its armored skull-like face.
  • Dark mist constantly emanates from gaps between its armor.
  • Its wings, if present, are small or vestigial rather than conventional dragon wings.

Drakoryn establishes the roster's imposing bipedal Dragon/Volt progression, while Vaelora provides the spectacular flying Legendary archetype. Nethryss should feel like a walking nightmare from beneath the earth.

Skeletal armor

The key distinction is that this isn't literally undead. Its skeleton has evolved externally reinforced structures, almost like an extreme biological armor system. So the ribs, spine and skull-like plates are living parts of its anatomy. That lets us retain the dragon's biological nature while giving it the horrifying skeletal aesthetic.

Umbral

Its presence suppresses surrounding light. Dark mist spills from its body. Shadows appear unnaturally elongated around it.

Dragon

Pure physical power and devastating Dragon energy. Its head is particularly intimidating: A huge horned skull-like face, glowing eyes recessed deep beneath the brow, and an enormous jaw filled with uneven dark fangs.

If someone sees Nethryss in the dark, they probably remember the experience for the rest of their life.

Not a robot. Not artificial. A living creature whose body naturally developed around metallic and crystalline structures.
