Leveling & Evolution System

1. Purpose

The Leveling and Evolution System governs how individual Entheon gain experience, increase in level, learn ordinary moves, and become eligible to evolve. Level and evolution are properties of the individual Entheon, while evolution requirements are defined by the species. The progression system tracks each individual Entheon’s progression.

2. Level System

Every individual Entheon has a Level from 1 to 100. Level represents the Entheon’s overall development, training and accumulated experience. Level affects: - Access to level-up moves. - Evolution eligibility. - General combat development. - Other species-specific progression where explicitly defined. Level does not automatically determine personality, Nature or Special Move/Ability.

3. Experience Points

The XP required to reach the next level follows the formula: > XP Required = 100 + (Current Level × 20) Examples:

When an Entheon gains enough XP to level up, any excess XP carries over toward the following level.

4. Sources of Experience

XP represents meaningful development rather than simply the number of opponents defeated.

4.1 Battle Experience

An Entheon can gain XP by: - Participating meaningfully in a battle. - Defeating an opponent. - Helping defeat an opponent. - Successfully overcoming a significantly stronger opponent. The amount of XP should consider: - The Entheon’s level. - The opponent’s level. - The relative difficulty of the encounter. - The Entheon’s actual participation. An Entheon should not receive the same reward for defeating a vastly weaker opponent as it would for overcoming a meaningful challenge.

4.2 Training Experience

Entheon can gain XP through meaningful training. Examples include: - Practicing moves. - Physical conditioning. - Learning to control an Affinity. - Training against a partner. - Successfully mastering a difficult technique. Training generally provides less XP than a genuinely challenging battle, but allows Entheon to develop without requiring constant combat.

4.3 Significant Experience

Meaningful story events can also contribute XP. Examples include: - Protecting a trainer or ally during a dangerous event. - Overcoming a significant personal obstacle. - Successfully applying something learned through previous training. - Surviving or overcoming a particularly difficult situation. XP should reflect meaningful development, not arbitrary narrative rewards.

5. Anti-Grinding Rule

Repeatedly defeating extremely weak opponents should provide progressively reduced XP. This prevents trivial encounters from becoming an efficient method of unlimited progression. Experience gained reflects the relative level and difficulty of the encounter. The system should reward meaningful challenges and development, not repetitive farming.

6. Level-Up Procedure

  1. Increase the Entheon’s Level by 1.
  2. Carry excess XP into the next level.
  3. Check the species’ Level-Up Learnset.
  4. Check whether an evolution requirement has been satisfied.
  5. Check for any other species-specific progression.
  6. Update the Entheon’s hidden progression state. If the new level corresponds to a move in the species’ Level-Up Learnset, that move becomes available for the Entheon to learn.

7. Evolution System

Evolution is a species-specific transformation that changes an Entheon into a predetermined evolutionary form. Each evolutionary species has a fixed evolution path. Evolution always follows the established species data. For example: > Nimblet → Nymbril → Nymbrake Nimblet cannot randomly evolve into another species.

8. Evolution Eligibility

Meeting an evolution requirement makes evolution available. It does not automatically force the evolution to occur. > [Entheon] is ready to evolve into Evolution. Would you like to proceed with its evolution? This should function similarly to the classic Entheon game mechanic where the player can choose whether to allow an evolution to proceed. The player may: - Proceed with evolution. - Decline/delay evolution. If evolution is delayed, the Entheon remains in its current form and can continue gaining levels normally. Once an evolution requirement has been satisfied, the requirement remains satisfied until the evolution is resolved. The player can choose to evolve the Entheon later.

10. Special Evolution Requirements

Not every Entheon evolves through ordinary level progression. Some species require additional conditions that reflect their established concepts.

10.1 Bond Evolution

Sairi → Sairune

Requirement:

Level 30 + High Bond Sairi must reach the required level while having developed a sufficiently strong bond with its trainer. Meeting the level requirement without sufficient Bond does not trigger evolution.

10.2 Item Evolution

Tivvi → Tivarra

Requirement:

Storm Shard The Storm Shard is a special evolutionary item that resonates with Tivvi’s Volt affinity. The item triggers the evolution when used appropriately.

10.3 Time-Based Evolution

Esvin → Esvar

Requirement:

Level 30 + Nighttime Esvin must reach the required level during the appropriate nighttime condition.

10.4 Bond + Level Evolution

Yori → Yorvale

Requirement:

Level 40 + High Bond Yori must reach Level 40 and have developed a sufficiently strong bond with its trainer.

10.5 Artifact Evolution

Orli → Orlith

Requirement:

Level 30 + Ancient Relic Orli requires an Ancient Relic in addition to reaching the required level. The Ancient Relic resonates with Orli’s artifact-like nature and enables its evolution.

10.6 Environmental Milestone Evolution

Rilsa → Rilsaryn

Requirement:

Level 32 + Radiant Garden exposure Rilsa must reach the required level and experience a meaningful exposure to a suitable Radiant Garden environment. The environmental requirement represents a developmental milestone rather than a simple location check.

11. Branching Evolution

Veyli Evolution

Veyli has three possible evolutionary forms: > Veyli → Veylith > Veyli → Veyrune > Veyli → Veyvara The evolution path is determined by the evolutionary catalyst used.

Veyli → Veylith

Requirement:

Veyli → Veyrune

Requirement:

Level 28 + Tide Pearl

12. Evolutionary Forms Are Fixed

Each species has a predetermined evolutionary relationship. The system never introduces an alternative evolutionary form unless that form has been explicitly established in the species database. Evolution therefore changes the Entheon’s species identity, rather than simply applying a generic upgrade.

13. Entheon That Do Not Evolve

Single-stage Entheon do not have an evolution requirement and cannot evolve. Their Level can still increase normally. Level progression can provide: - New moves. - Combat development. - Other explicitly defined species progression. The following are permanently single-stage: - All established one-stage Entheon. - All established Legendary Entheon. Legendaries are therefore not expected to evolve.

14. Delayed Evolution

A trainer may deliberately delay an available evolution. An Entheon that has declined evolution: - Remains in its current form. - Continues gaining XP normally. - Continues learning moves normally. - Retains its accumulated XP and Level. - May evolve later when the trainer chooses to proceed. If an Entheon has multiple possible evolutionary branches, the trainer’s choice of catalyst determines the branch when evolution is eventually performed.

15. Evolution and Learned Moves

  1. Update the Entheon’s species.
  2. Preserve its existing Level.
  3. Preserve its accumulated XP.
  4. Preserve its Nature.
  5. Preserve its Personality and established Individual Quirks unless the story naturally develops them.
  6. Preserve an existing Special Move/Ability.
  7. Check the new species’ Level-Up Learnset.
  8. Add any newly available moves appropriate to the Entheon’s current level.
  9. Update the Entheon’s evolution state. Evolution does not reset the Entheon’s Level or XP.

16. Special Move/Ability and Evolution

Special Moves/Abilities are separate from ordinary moves. If an individual Entheon already possesses a Special Move/Ability before evolving: > The Special Move/Ability is retained through evolution unless its established description explicitly states otherwise. Evolution does not automatically grant a Special Move/Ability to an individual that did not previously possess one. Therefore: > Individual A evolves while possessing a Special Ability → retains it. > Individual B evolves without a Special Ability → does not automatically gain one merely because it evolved. This preserves individual variation within the same species.

Example

Nimblet has reached Level 16. Evolution requirement satisfied: Nimblet → Nymbril “Nimblet is ready to evolve into Nymbril. Would you like to proceed?” If the player chooses No, Nimblet remains Nimblet. If the player later chooses Yes, the evolution proceeds.

18. Design Principle

Evolution should feel like a meaningful development milestone rather than an automatic statistical upgrade. The system therefore combines: - Level - Experience - Training - Bond - Items - Time - Environment - Player choice Different Entheon can therefore have different evolutionary journeys while remaining completely deterministic within their established species rules.
