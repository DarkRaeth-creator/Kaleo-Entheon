Special Move & Ability System

1. Purpose

Special Moves and Special Abilities are distinctive traits that give individual Entheon additional mechanical identity beyond their standard move pool, Affinity and statistics. A Special does not automatically mean an Entheon is stronger. A Special may provide: - Offensive effects - Defensive effects - Recovery - Battlefield control - Mobility - Utility - Status interaction - Affinity interaction - Support - Environmental interaction - A unique passive effect Some Specials may be highly powerful in specific situations, while others may be subtle but useful.

2. Individual Variation

Special Moves and Special Abilities are not automatically possessed by every Entheon of a species. A species may have a designated Special Move or Special Ability that an individual Entheon can possess, without every member of that species having it. This creates meaningful individual variation between Entheon of the same species. For example: > Tarnelle A — Stone/Metal — No Special >  Tarnelle B — Stone/Metal — Stoneheart Both are valid Tarnelle. Their other characteristics may also differ. A trainer may therefore encounter a wild Entheon that possesses a Special while their own Entheon of the same species does not. This can create meaningful reasons to consider catching and training another individual of a species.

3. Special Acquisition Through Evolution

For ordinary evolutionary lines, gaining a Special upon evolution is possible but not guaranteed. An Entheon that evolves into a form eligible for a species-specific Special may: - Gain the Special upon evolution; or - Evolve without gaining the Special. The Special is therefore an individual trait, not an automatic evolutionary reward.

Existing Special

If an Entheon already possesses a Special before evolving, that Special is retained. When the Special has an evolved-form version, the existing Special may develop into that evolved version. Example: > Tarnelle with Stoneheart >  ↓ Evolution >  Tarnovar with Living Bastion However: > Tarnelle without a Special >  ↓ Evolution >  Tarnovar without a Special is also possible.

4. Starter Exception

The three starter Entheon are a deliberate exception to the normal acquisition rules. Each starter line has a designated Special Ability from Stage 1. The starter’s Special: - Is present from the beginning. - Is retained through evolution. - May develop or become more sophisticated with each evolution. The three starter lines therefore begin with an immediately recognizable mechanical identity. This exception applies specifically to the three starter species and does not establish a general rule that all Stage 1 Entheon possess Specials.

5. Common Entheon

Common Entheon generally do not begin with a Special Move or Special Ability. For Common Entheon belonging to evolutionary lines: - Stage 1 normally has no Special. - An evolved form may gain its species’ designated Special. - Gaining that Special upon evolution is not guaranteed. - An individual that already possesses a Special retains it when evolving. The purpose is to make Special acquisition a potential discovery rather than an automatic part of evolution.

6. Uncommon Entheon

Uncommon Entheon may possess a Special Move or Special Ability. For evolutionary lines, the Special may first appear upon evolution. Possession remains individual rather than guaranteed unless the species is specifically defined otherwise.

7. Rare Entheon

Rare Entheon are generally more likely to possess distinctive Specials. Rare evolutionary forms may have a designated Special that an individual can acquire upon reaching that evolutionary stage. However, rarity alone does not guarantee possession unless explicitly stated for that species.

8. Extremely Rare Entheon

Extremely Rare Entheon normally have a distinctive Special Move or Special Ability associated with their species. Their Special does not automatically need to be overwhelmingly powerful. It may instead provide an unusual mechanic, strong utility, battlefield control, support, defense or another defining characteristic.

9. Legendary Entheon

Legendary Entheon always possess their defining Special Move or Special Ability. A Legendary Special should reinforce the identity of the Legendary Entheon rather than simply functioning as an extremely powerful attack. A Legendary Special may be: - Offensive - Defensive - Supportive - Environmental - Utility-based - Passive - Battlefield-oriented - Or a combination of these Legendary status does not require the Special itself to be an offensive powerhouse.

10. Discovering Specials

Specials should be discoverable through observation and interaction. A trainer should be able to recognize when an Entheon demonstrates an unusual Special during battle. This allows wild encounters to create meaningful moments where a trainer realizes that an individual Entheon possesses a particular Special. For example: > A wild Tarnelle withstands an attack and its rocky body visibly hardens. The trainer can recognize that this Tarnelle possesses Stoneheart. This prevents Special acquisition from becoming purely invisible randomness while still allowing individual Entheon to differ.

Special Ability / Move Roster

The following roster defines the designated Special concept for eligible Entheon. It does not mean every individual of that species automatically possesses the Special.

001–003 — Nimblet → Nymbril → Nymbrake

Wild → Wild/Gale

001 — Nimblet

Special Ability: Improvised Instinct

Nimblet instinctively exploits its surroundings when threatened. Terrain, nearby objects and positioning can influence how effectively it evades or responds to an attack. Note: Nimblet is a starter and therefore receives its Special from Stage 1.

002 — Nymbril

Special Ability: Improvised Instinct

The ability develops alongside Nymbril’s increasing agility. It can deliberately use terrain and short bursts of Gale energy to alter its position after an opponent commits to an attack.

003 — Nymbrake

Special Ability: Improvised Instinct

Nymbrake has mastered environmental improvisation. Its movement can incorporate terrain, momentum and Gale energy to turn the battlefield itself into part of its fighting style.

004–006 — Pipiri → Pirello → Piravelle

Tide → Tide/Frost

004 — Pipiri

Special Ability: Hydrokinetic Recovery

Pipiri can draw upon surrounding moisture to restore a small amount of vitality after successfully using Tide techniques. Note: Pipiri is a starter and therefore receives its Special from Stage 1.

005 — Pirello

Special Ability: Hydrokinetic Recovery

The ability develops to incorporate Frost: water-based techniques can leave behind small amounts of restorative crystalline energy.

006 — Piravelle

Special Ability: Frozen Renewal

Piravelle can manipulate the relationship between water and temperature to create restorative crystalline formations, allowing its Tide/Frost techniques to provide limited recovery or protection.

007–009 — Morrowe → Morveth → Morvayne

Umbral → Umbral/Mystic

007 — Morrowe

Special Ability: Veiled Presence

Morrowe becomes difficult to detect when it remains still or moves through darkness. Note: Morrowe is a starter and therefore receives its Special from Stage 1.

008 — Morveth

Special Ability: Veiled Presence

Morveth can deliberately suppress its presence, causing opponents to have greater difficulty determining its exact position.

009 — Morvayne

Special Ability: Veiled Presence

Morvayne combines Umbral concealment with Mystic distortion, allowing it to briefly make opponents perceive its position incorrectly.

010–012 — Kivvi → Kivara → Kivarune

Radiant → Radiant/Frost

010 — Kivvi

No designated Special

Kivvi does not normally possess a Special at this stage.

011 — Kivara

Special Ability: Ethereal Drift

Kivara’s increasingly refined body can disperse deliberately, allowing it to evade or mitigate attacks while leaving behind a brief trail of Radiant/Frost energy.

012 — Kivarune

Special Ability: Celestial Drift

Kivarune can momentarily become almost entirely ethereal, allowing it to slip through an attack while shedding radiant light and crystalline Frost particles.

013–015 — Brindlew → Brindrel → Brinderv

Verdant → Verdant/Stone

013 — Brindlew

No designated Special

Brindlew does not normally possess a Special at this stage.

014 — Brindrel

Special Ability: Persistent Growth

Its vegetation becomes tougher and can interact with Stone techniques, creating small rooted barriers or obstacles.

015 — Brinderv

Special Ability: Rootbound Strength

Brinderv can combine its mature vegetation with its Stone body to create temporary rooted structures that hinder opponents and reinforce its position.

016–018 — Sovel → Sovelle → Sovaryn

Gale → Gale/Radiant

016 — Sovel

No designated Special

Sovel does not normally possess a Special at this stage.

017 — Sovelle

Special Ability: Slipstream

Sovelle can deliberately create a localized current around itself, improving aerial maneuverability and allowing it to change direction sharply.

018 — Sovaryn

Special Ability: Skycurrent

Sovaryn effectively “swims” through the atmosphere, manipulating surrounding air currents to change direction, speed and trajectory with exceptional precision.

019–021 — Tarnit → Tarnelle → Tarnovar

Stone → Stone/Metal

019 — Tarnit

No designated Special

Tarnit does not normally possess a Special at this stage.

020 — Tarnelle

Special Ability: Stoneheart

Tarnelle can consciously reinforce portions of its Stone/Metal body when bracing against an incoming attack.

021 — Tarnovar

Special Ability: Living Bastion

Tarnovar can lock its stone and metal formations together, becoming exceptionally difficult to displace while braced.

022–024 — Quiblet → Quivane → Quivaryn

Flame → Flame/Volt

022 — Quiblet

No designated Special

Quiblet does not normally possess a Special at this stage.

023 — Quivane

The intensified Flame begins generating electrical charge through its plumage, allowing Flame and Volt energy to reinforce one another.

024 — Quivaryn

Special Ability: Stormfire

Quivaryn’s Flame and Volt energies become intertwined. Powerful aerial movements can build a temporary charge that enhances its next elemental technique.

026 — Elvara

Elvara’s blessings can provide a brief protective effect.

Special Ability: Sacred Benediction

Elvarin can imbue restorative and supportive techniques with a refined Mystic/Radiant blessing that may heal, cleanse or protect depending on the technique used.

028 — Drakyn

No designated Special

030 — Drakoryn

The fully evolved Dragon can harmonize Dragon and Volt energy through its entire body, temporarily improving the interaction between its two affinities after a sufficiently powerful impact.

032 — Vessari

Tide/Dragon — Rare

Special Ability: Serpentine Grace

Vessari’s elegant body allows it to manipulate surrounding water as an extension of its own movement. While in water, its movements become increasingly difficult to predict, allowing it to redirect incoming attacks or reposition without sacrificing momentum.

036 — Meliora

Verdant/Radiant — Rare

Special Ability: Bloom of Light

When Meliora uses Verdant techniques that cause plants or flowers to grow, those plants can absorb Radiant energy and temporarily produce a secondary beneficial effect. This could eventually become healing, protection or purification, depending on the specific move.

046 — Braskel

Flame/Stone — Rare

Special Ability: Volcanic Heart

Braskel’s internal volcanic core stores heat generated during battle. When sufficiently charged, it can release that stored energy through a Flame or Stone technique, producing an enhanced secondary effect.

050 — Tivarra

Volt/Radiant — Rare

Special Ability: Luminous Charge

Tivarra can convert stored electrical energy into Radiant energy. When sufficiently charged, its wings become intensely luminous and its next Radiant technique gains an additional battlefield effect.

052 — Cairneth

Stone/Mystic — Rare

Special Ability: Arcane Orbit

Cairneth’s orbiting stones can independently respond to threats, briefly forming geometric defensive arrangements or barriers around its central core.

054 — Lumaryn

Radiant/Mystic — Rare

Special Ability: Celestial Array

Lumaryn’s radiant appendages can rearrange themselves into different formations, changing the way its Radiant energy manifests—such as concentrated beams, protective illumination or distributed light.

060 — Esvar

Umbral/Metal — Rare

Special Ability: Spectral Armament

Esvar can temporarily rearrange its floating metallic components into different configurations, allowing it to create defensive structures, blades or barriers while remaining partially intangible.

062 — Marnyx

Tide/Wild — Rare

Special Ability: Undertow Strength

Marnyx’s powerful body can generate tremendous water displacement through physical movement. Its physical attacks made in water can create secondary currents that alter positioning.

Dragon → Dragon/Radiant

063 — Yori

Special Ability: Raptor Instinct

Yori’s exceptional predatory instincts allow it to recognize openings in an opponent’s movement, improving its ability to exploit brief opportunities after an opponent misses or exposes itself.

064 — Yorvale

Special Ability: Celestial Presence

Yorvale’s radiant aura surrounds its imposing quadrupedal form. Opponents that attack Yorvale recklessly can find their momentum disrupted by the intense Radiant pressure surrounding it.

Mystic → Mystic/Frost

Common → Rare

065 — Thessa

No designated Special

066 — Thessane

Thessane’s refined Mystic abilities allow it to perceive and interpret subtle changes in energy around it. This can allow it to anticipate imminent attacks or detect hidden presences, while its Frost affinity can manifest that perception as crystalline patterns or brief freezing barriers. Design intent: Thessane develops from a curious psychic creature into an elegant mystical Frost oracle. Its ability combines Mystic perception and Frost manipulation rather than simply becoming a stronger version of Thessa’s telekinesis.

074 — Rilsaryn

Rilsaryn can draw water into its floral structures, causing its flowers to temporarily bloom at full strength. While fully bloomed, its Verdant and Tide techniques gain enhanced secondary effects.

076 — Uveryn

Mystic/Dragon — Extremely Rare

Special Ability: Astral Coil

Uveryn’s body generates enormous rings of Mystic energy as it moves. These rings can distort the trajectory of attacks or allow Uveryn to redirect its own movement through the air.

080 — Veyvara

Mystic — Rare

Special Ability: Psychic Dominion

Veyvara’s intense Mystic focus allows it to manipulate multiple small objects simultaneously. When sufficiently focused, it can use those objects as extensions of its own movement and positioning.

085 — Sindra

Flame/Umbral — Rare

Special Ability: Shadowflame

Sindra’s Flame does not behave like ordinary fire. When she uses Flame energy, portions of the fire can momentarily disappear into shadow before reappearing from a different angle.

095 — Veysha

Mystic/Umbral — Rare

Special Ability: Separated Shadow

Veysha can temporarily separate from its own shadow, creating a remote shadow-presence that can scout or interact with the battlefield before returning to its body.

097–101 — Legendary Entheon

097 — Auralith

Radiant/Mystic — Legendary

Special Ability: Living Monolith

Auralith’s immense body generates a stable field of Radiant/Mystic energy. While stationary, its floating monolithic fragments gradually arrange themselves into increasingly complex formations that can provide different defensive or supportive effects.

098 — Vaelora

Radiant/Volt — Legendary

Special Ability: Stormheart

Vaelora draws energy from atmospheric electricity while airborne. As electrical energy accumulates, its Radiant aura intensifies, allowing it to alternate between electrical and Radiant manifestations.

099 — Nethryss

Umbral/Dragon — Legendary

Special Ability: Absolute Presence

Passive Special Ability

Nethryss’s overwhelming presence imposes a profound instinctive fear upon opposing Entheon.

Core effect:

 Lowers opposing Attack even against Entheon that are normally immune to standard intimidation-based Attack reduction.

Special Rule:

Absolute Presence bypasses immunity to standard Attack-lowering intimidation effects. This exception applies only to Absolute Presence.

100 — Orrytheon

Mystic/Metal — Legendary

Special Ability: Astral Alignment

Orrytheon’s metallic rings and orbiting structures continuously adjust around its body. When it uses Mystic or Metal techniques, the rings can temporarily realign to alter the technique’s defensive or offensive characteristics.

101 — Kaelros

Umbral/Flame — Legendary

Special Ability: Wispwalk

Kaelros can manifest several dark-flame wisps around the battlefield and use them as temporary anchors for its movement.

Core effect: Kaelros can dissolve into Umbral energy and reappear at the location of one of its active wisps, allowing it to reposition without travelling through the space between the two points.

The originating wisp briefly collapses when Kaelros relocates, while the remaining wisps continue to drift around the battlefield. The ability is a spatial movement technique rather than conventional teleportation: Kaelros must first establish or maintain a wisp at the destination.

Design intent: Wispwalk gives Kaelros a distinctive battlefield identity built around mobility, misdirection and positional control rather than simply increasing its damage output.

Special System Design Principles

The Special system should follow these principles: 1. A Special is not guaranteed merely because a species has a designated Special. 2. Specials are individual traits. 3. Evolution can reveal a Special, but evolution does not guarantee one. 4. If an individual already has a Special, evolution does not remove it. 5. A Special may evolve or become more sophisticated when its owner evolves. 6. The three starter Entheon are the explicit Stage 1 exception. 7. Common Stage 1 Entheon normally have no Special. 8. Rare, Extremely Rare and Legendary Entheon are increasingly likely to possess distinctive Specials, but only Legendary Entheon are guaranteed to possess their defining Special. 9. A Special does not inherently indicate greater combat power. 10. Specials should create meaningful tactical or individual differences rather than simply increase damage. 11. Special activation mechanics, frequency, duration, stacking and numerical effects must be defined separately during the balancing phase. 12. A trainer should be able to recognize a Special when an individual Entheon demonstrates it in battle. 13. Different individuals of the same species may therefore have meaningfully different mechanical identities. 14. A trainer may have a legitimate reason to catch another individual of a species if it demonstrates a Special their current Entheon lacks. 15. The system should avoid rewarding trainers for deliberately allowing their Entheon to take unnecessary damage solely to activate a Special.
