7. Affinity in the World

Affinity is one of the fundamental characteristics of Entheon. It influences an Entheon’s biology, natural abilities and combat identity, but it does not determine its personality, intelligence, temperament or morality. An Affinity should be understood as a natural characteristic of an Entheon rather than a simple classification of its behaviour.

7.1 What Affinity Represents

Every Entheon possesses one or two Affinities. An Affinity represents the type of energy, phenomenon or natural force with which an Entheon is inherently associated. Affinity can influence:

  • an Entheon’s physical and biological characteristics
  • naturally occurring abilities
  • the techniques it can learn or use
  • its interactions with other Affinities
  • the environments in which it naturally thrives
  • its overall combat identity

An Entheon’s Affinity does not completely determine its appearance or behaviour. Two Entheon with the same Affinity can look, behave and fight very differently. Likewise, an Entheon’s Affinity does not determine its personality or morality. A creature with an Umbral Affinity is not inherently sinister, just as a creature with a Radiant Affinity is not inherently benevolent.

7.2 The Thirteen Affinities

The world recognizes thirteen established Affinities:

  • Flame
  • Tide
  • Verdant
  • Gale
  • Volt
  • Frost
  • Stone
  • Metal
  • Mystic
  • Radiant
  • Umbral
  • Wild
  • Dragon

Affinities can exist individually or in combination. Dual-Affinity Entheon possess characteristics associated with both Affinities. The combination does not necessarily mean that both Affinities are equally expressed in every aspect of the Entheon’s biology or abilities.

7.3 Affinity and Humans

Affinity is primarily an Entheon characteristic. Humans do not naturally possess an Entheon’s Affinity simply because they live alongside or train Entheon of that Affinity. Human interaction with Affinity is primarily technological, academic and practical rather than equivalent to an Entheon’s innate Affinity. Whether humans can naturally manifest Affinity themselves, and the extent to which this is possible, remains a subject of study rather than an assumed universal human ability.

7.4 Affinity in the Environment

Affinity is not restricted to individual Entheon. Certain environments naturally contain stronger concentrations or manifestations of particular Affinities. This contributes to the distribution of Entheon species throughout the world and helps explain why particular species are more commonly encountered in some regions than others. Examples include:

  • Volt activity around electrical infrastructure and naturally charged environments
  • Tide phenomena around oceans, lakes, rivers and other bodies of water
  • Flame phenomena in volcanic or geothermally active environments
  • Frost phenomena in cold northern environments
  • Verdant phenomena in forests and other biologically rich environments
  • Stone phenomena in mountainous, rocky and subterranean environments
  • Gale phenomena in exposed highlands, coastlines and other areas of strong atmospheric movement
  • Metal phenomena around mineral-rich regions and areas of significant metalworking or industrial activity
  • Mystic phenomena around locations with unusual concentrations of anomalous or psychic energy
  • Radiant phenomena associated with intense light, celestial or spiritually significant environments
  • Umbral phenomena associated with darkness, deep underground environments and other areas where light is naturally limited
  • Wild phenomena associated with untamed ecosystems and primal natural environments
  • Dragon phenomena associated with particularly powerful or unusual concentrations of Affinity energy

These environmental associations do not mean that an Affinity can only exist in its preferred environment. Rare Entheon can occur outside their typical habitats, and unusual environmental conditions can produce unexpected Affinity concentrations. Particularly strong or unusual Affinity phenomena may also occur around ancient sites, shrines, caves and other locations with unusual environmental or historical characteristics.

7.5 Affinity and Society

Affinities have developed cultural associations throughout society. Certain Affinities may be commonly associated with particular professions, activities or cultural traditions. A person working with electrical infrastructure may frequently work alongside Volt Entheon, while explorers in northern regions may commonly partner with Frost Entheon. These associations are practical and cultural rather than deterministic. Society does not universally treat Affinities as indicators of personality or character. An individual Entheon’s behaviour is determined by the individual, not simply by its Affinity. Nevertheless, stereotypes can exist. Some people may associate Umbral with secrecy, Flame with aggression, Mystic with intelligence or Radiant with nobility. Such assumptions are cultural perceptions rather than established truths.

7.6 Affinity Research

Affinity is a significant area of scientific research. Researchers study:

  • how Affinity develops
  • why Entheon possess particular Affinities
  • how dual Affinities interact
  • how Affinity relates to biology
  • how Affinity manifests in the environment
  • how Affinity influences Entheon abilities
  • how Affinity can be measured and detected
  • how Affinity can interact with technology

Much is understood about the practical effects of Affinity, particularly its applications in battling and technology. However, the underlying nature and origin of Affinity remain subjects of ongoing research. Ancient records and unusual Affinity phenomena occasionally provide evidence that challenges current scientific understanding.

7.7 Affinity in Everyday Life

Affinity has applications far beyond battling. Communities make practical use of Affinity-related technology and Entheon abilities in areas such as:

  • energy generation
  • construction
  • agriculture
  • transportation
  • environmental management
  • mining
  • manufacturing
  • communications
  • emergency services
  • entertainment and sport

The extent of Affinity use varies between regions according to local resources, technology and the Entheon species naturally found there. Affinity therefore forms part of ordinary life rather than existing solely as a combat mechanic.

7.8 Affinity and Battle

Affinity is a fundamental component of Entheon combat. An Entheon’s Affinity influences the techniques it can learn, the effectiveness of techniques used against it and the way its natural abilities interact with other Affinities. Using a technique that matches one of an Entheon’s own Affinities provides the Same-Affinity Bonus. For dual-Affinity Entheon, either of their Affinities can provide the bonus when matched by the technique. Affinity effectiveness is determined by the established combat system and includes multiple levels of effectiveness rather than a simple effective/ineffective binary. The complete Affinity interaction chart is maintained as part of the Creature Combat System rather than being duplicated here. Affinity provides the foundation for combat interactions, but it does not determine the outcome of a battle by itself. Trainer strategy, individual Entheon capabilities, abilities, techniques, statistics, positioning and circumstances all remain relevant.

7.9 Affinity Does Not Define the Individual

Affinity is an important part of an Entheon’s identity, but it is only one part. An Entheon’s Affinity does not determine:

  • personality
  • intelligence
  • morality
  • temperament
  • loyalty
  • Bond with its Trainer
  • preferred behaviour
  • willingness to battle

A Flame Entheon can be gentle. An Umbral Entheon can be affectionate. A Radiant Entheon can be mischievous. A Mystic Entheon can be stubborn. A Wild Entheon can be highly intelligent and disciplined. The Affinity describes what an Entheon is naturally aligned with, not who that individual Entheon is.
