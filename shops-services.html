14. Shops & Services

Kaleo has a broad network of shops and services supporting everyday life, Entheon ownership and Trainer activities. The availability and specialization of services varies according to the size and importance of a settlement. Major cities provide the widest range of options, while smaller communities generally focus on essential goods and services.

14.1 General Retail

Ordinary retail is a normal part of life throughout Kaleo. Depending on the size of the settlement, people may have access to:

  • supermarkets and grocery stores
  • clothing shops
  • electronics retailers
  • household goods
  • pharmacies and personal-care stores
  • specialty shops
  • convenience stores
  • restaurants and food vendors

Major cities contain large commercial districts and specialized retailers, while smaller settlements generally provide a more limited selection.

14.2 Entheon Supplies

Specialized shops provide products for Entheon care and training. Common products include:

  • Entheon food
  • treats and supplements
  • grooming products
  • toys and enrichment equipment
  • training equipment
  • protective equipment
  • battle supplies
  • Capture Crystals
  • Entheon accessories

Products vary considerably in quality and price. Basic supplies are widely accessible, while specialized training equipment and premium products can be considerably more expensive.

14.3 Medical and Entheon Healthcare

Healthcare is widely available throughout Kaleo. Human medical facilities range from small local clinics to large hospitals and specialist medical centres. Entheon healthcare operates alongside human medicine and includes:

  • routine examinations
  • treatment of injuries
  • post-battle recovery
  • preventative care
  • nutritional guidance
  • rehabilitation
  • specialist treatment
  • emergency care

Major cities generally contain specialized Entheon medical facilities, while smaller settlements may rely on local clinics or refer patients to larger centres. Entheon healthcare is treated as a professional medical field rather than simply an extension of Trainer services.

14.4 Capture Crystal Services

Capture Crystals are widely available through authorized retailers and specialist suppliers. Services associated with Capture Crystals may include:

  • purchasing new Capture Crystals
  • Crystal identification
  • maintenance and inspection
  • repair of damaged equipment
  • registration services
  • specialized Crystal fitting and configuration

Standard Capture Crystals are affordable enough for ordinary Trainers, while specialized or high-performance models can be significantly more expensive. Capture Crystal technology is regulated, particularly where modifications could affect Entheon safety or violate established capture laws.

14.5 Accommodation and Hospitality

Kaleo has a wide range of accommodation options. Depending on the location, travellers may find:

  • hotels
  • guesthouses
  • hostels
  • inns
  • private rentals
  • resorts
  • campsites

Many establishments are accustomed to accommodating Trainers and their Entheon. Some provide dedicated Entheon facilities such as:

  • secure resting areas
  • food preparation areas
  • outdoor exercise spaces
  • grooming facilities
  • temporary Entheon accommodation

Major cities and tourist destinations generally offer the widest range of accommodation.

14.6 Food and Restaurants

Food culture varies significantly between regions. Restaurants, cafés, markets, food stalls and other establishments are common throughout Kaleo. Trainers travelling with Entheon can generally purchase appropriate food for themselves and their partners, although specialized Entheon diets may require visiting dedicated suppliers. Regional cuisine reflects local ingredients, climate, culture and economic activity.

14.7 Trainer Services

Larger settlements commonly provide specialized services aimed at Trainers. These may include:

  • training facilities
  • public battle arenas
  • private practice facilities
  • Trainer coaching
  • Entheon grooming
  • equipment rental
  • specialized training programs
  • performance and conditioning services

Some services are highly specialized and may only be available in major cities. Trainers are not required to use professional facilities. Public spaces and natural environments can also be used for training where local regulations permit it.

14.8 Financial Services

Modern financial services are widely available. People may use:

  • banks
  • ATMs
  • digital payment systems
  • electronic transfers
  • payment cards
  • mobile payment services

Trainers can receive prize money, wages and other payments through standard financial services. Major cities contain larger financial institutions and more specialized services, while smaller communities generally provide basic banking access.

14.9 Communication Services

Kaleo has advanced communication infrastructure. People commonly use mobile devices and other connected technology for:

  • communication
  • navigation
  • banking
  • entertainment
  • emergency services
  • Trainer records
  • accessing public information

Communication coverage is extensive around developed areas but can become less reliable in remote wilderness.

14.10 Regional Availability

Services scale with settlement size and importance. Major cities can provide almost any ordinary commercial or professional service. Towns generally provide essential services along with a selection of specialized businesses. Villages and small settlements focus primarily on everyday necessities. Remote communities may have only limited shops and services and can rely on larger settlements for specialized goods, medical care, advanced equipment and other needs. This variation means that Trainers travelling through Kaleo need to plan ahead when entering remote areas.
