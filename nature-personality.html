Nature & Personality System

1. Purpose

The Nature and Personality System determines the individual behavioral characteristics of an Entheon. It exists to ensure that Entheon of the same species can still develop distinct personalities, relationships and behavioral patterns. Nature and Personality are not combat statistics and do not inherently determine an Entheon’s strength. An Entheon’s: - Species - Affinity - Physical design - Species traits - Rarity - Special Move or Special Ability - Nature - Personality - Individual quirks are separate concepts and should not be treated as interchangeable.

2. Nature

Nature is the Entheon’s primary behavioral tendency. Each individual Entheon should normally have one primary Nature selected from the established Nature list. Nature describes a broad behavioral inclination rather than determining every aspect of the Entheon’s personality. For example: > Nature: Curious A Curious Entheon is naturally inclined to investigate unfamiliar objects, people and situations. However, its individual personality may determine how that curiosity manifests. A Curious Entheon could be: - Shy and hesitant - Bold and adventurous - Mischievous and intrusive - Calm and observant - Affectionate and eager to investigate alongside its trainer Nature therefore establishes a foundation rather than a rigid character archetype.

3. Nature List

Brave

Courageous and willing to face danger. A Brave Entheon is inclined to confront threats rather than retreat from them.

Timid

Cautious and easily startled. A Timid Entheon may hesitate around unfamiliar people, situations or dangers. Timidity does not prevent bravery when something important is at stake.

Calm

Composed and emotionally steady. A Calm Entheon rarely panics and generally responds to unexpected situations without excessive reaction.

Bold

Confident and assertive. A Bold Entheon is comfortable challenging others and is unlikely to back down simply because an opponent appears stronger.

Gentle

Kind and non-aggressive. A Gentle Entheon generally avoids unnecessary conflict and tends to handle other creatures carefully.

Proud

Self-confident and dignified. A Proud Entheon dislikes being underestimated, patronized or treated as inferior.

Shy

Reserved and socially cautious. A Shy Entheon may require considerable time to become comfortable around unfamiliar people or Entheon.

Curious

Fascinated by the unfamiliar. A Curious Entheon naturally investigates strange objects, unfamiliar environments and unusual situations.

Playful

Energetic and fun-loving. A Playful Entheon enjoys games, interaction and harmless forms of entertainment.

Mischievous

Enjoys tricks and harmless trouble. A Mischievous Entheon may play pranks, steal objects temporarily or deliberately provoke amusing reactions.

Affectionate

Seeks closeness and emotional connection. An Affectionate Entheon enjoys praise, attention and physical affection from trusted companions.

Loyal

Forms strong attachments to companions. A Loyal Entheon remains close to its trainer and allies and places considerable importance on established bonds.

Protective

Prioritizes the safety of others. A Protective Entheon may instinctively place itself between danger and a companion.

Independent

Prefers acting on its own judgment. An Independent Entheon may sometimes make decisions without waiting for explicit instructions, particularly when it believes it understands the situation.

Competitive

Enjoys challenges and rivalry. A Competitive Entheon is motivated by opportunities to test itself against others.

Serious

Focused and disciplined. A Serious Entheon tends to treat training, battles and important situations with considerable attention.

Energetic

Constantly active and enthusiastic. An Energetic Entheon rarely remains still for long and frequently initiates activities.

Relaxed

Easygoing and difficult to bother. A Relaxed Entheon generally takes setbacks and unexpected situations in stride.

Stubborn

Resistant to changing its mind. A Stubborn Entheon may continue pursuing an idea or course of action even after being told to stop.

Determined

Highly persistent toward goals. A Determined Entheon strongly dislikes giving up after failure and will continue attempting to overcome obstacles.

Clever

Observant and analytical. A Clever Entheon tends to look for solutions, patterns and opportunities rather than acting immediately.

Cautious

Methodical and risk-aware. A Cautious Entheon considers possible consequences before committing to an action.

Adventurous

Thrilled by exploration. An Adventurous Entheon actively seeks unfamiliar experiences and enjoys discovering new places.

Empathetic

Highly sensitive to the emotions of others. An Empathetic Entheon notices changes in the emotional state of companions and may react strongly to them.

Dignified

Naturally composed and formal. A Dignified Entheon carries itself with unusual grace and restraint.

Inquisitive

Driven to understand how things work. An Inquisitive Entheon observes, experiments and investigates mechanisms, objects and phenomena.

Free-Spirited

Strongly dislikes unnecessary restrictions. A Free-Spirited Entheon prefers freedom, improvisation and making its own choices.

Clingy

Forms exceptionally strong attachments. A Clingy Entheon strongly prefers remaining near its chosen companion and may actively seek reassurance or attention.

Stoic

Rarely displays emotion outwardly. A Stoic Entheon may care deeply about others while showing little obvious emotional reaction.

Fierce

Intense and forceful when challenged. A Fierce Entheon can become highly intimidating when confronted or when protecting something important.

Gentle-Hearted

Highly compassionate and sensitive to suffering. A Gentle-Hearted Entheon may become distressed when another creature is injured or upset.

Sly

Clever and opportunistic. A Sly Entheon naturally looks for openings and enjoys outsmarting opponents or finding unconventional solutions.

Serene

Naturally tranquil. A Serene Entheon appears unusually peaceful and is difficult to rush or provoke.

Excitable

Emotions quickly reach the surface. An Excitable Entheon reacts enthusiastically to interesting events and may display its feelings very openly.

Observant

Prefers watching before acting. An Observant Entheon frequently notices details that others overlook and may spend considerable time studying a situation before responding.

Devoted

Forms exceptionally deep bonds. A Devoted Entheon may go to considerable lengths for its chosen companions and can develop particularly strong emotional attachments.

4. Personality Expression

Nature establishes the primary behavioral tendency, but it does not completely define the individual Entheon. An Entheon should normally have one primary Nature and may receive one to three Personality Expressions. Personality Expressions describe how the Nature manifests in that individual.

Example

Nature: Timid  Personality Expressions: Affectionate, Observant Possible result: > A cautious Entheon that tends to hide behind its trainer around strangers, but closely watches everything happening around it. Once it trusts someone, it becomes surprisingly affectionate. Another individual could have: Nature: Timid  Personality Expressions: Independent, Clever Possible result: > A cautious Entheon that avoids confrontation but prefers solving problems alone rather than relying on its trainer. Both are Timid Entheon, but they are clearly different individuals.

5. Personality Expressions Are Not Additional Natures

Personality Expressions should not be treated as additional full Natures. They exist to customize how the primary Nature manifests. For example: > Nature: Proud >  Personality Expression: Affectionate does not create a contradiction. The Entheon may be dignified and dislike appearing weak while secretly enjoying praise and affection from trusted companions. Similarly: > Nature: Timid >  Personality Expression: Brave should not automatically change the Nature to Brave. Instead, it may describe an Entheon that is normally cautious but becomes courageous when protecting its trainer. Personality should therefore be capable of producing apparent contradictions. These contradictions help individual Entheon feel like actual characters rather than fixed archetypes.

6. Individual Quirks

An Entheon may additionally develop one or more Individual Quirks. Quirks are small behavioral details that make an individual memorable. Examples include: - Loves shiny objects. - Sleeps curled around its trainer’s bag. - Hates being wet. - Always tries to sit on the highest available object. - Makes small noises when concentrating. - Becomes extremely competitive about eating. - Collects interesting stones. - Copies its trainer’s gestures. - Gets embarrassed when praised. - Falls asleep during long conversations. - Constantly rearranges objects around its sleeping area. - Likes being carried despite being physically capable of walking. - Becomes fascinated by mirrors. - Always investigates new smells. - Has a favorite place to sleep. Individual Quirks should generally have no direct mechanical effect unless explicitly established elsewhere. Their purpose is characterization.

7. Species and Personality

Species characteristics may influence which Natures and Personality Expressions are plausible, but they must never determine personality automatically. An Entheon’s Affinity, appearance, species lore or rarity should not be treated as a personality shortcut. For example: - A Flame Entheon does not automatically have a fiery or aggressive personality. - An Umbral Entheon does not automatically have a sinister personality. - A Radiant Entheon does not automatically have a kind personality. - A Dragon Entheon does not automatically have a proud or dominant personality. - A Mystic Entheon does not automatically have a calm or intelligent personality. Species descriptions provide context, not restrictions. Two Entheon of the same species may have substantially different personalities.

8. Affinity and Personality

Affinity should remain mechanically and narratively separate from personality. An Entheon’s Affinity describes its elemental nature and abilities. It does not determine how the Entheon thinks or behaves. Examples: > Flame + Timid A timid Flame Entheon may be easily startled despite possessing Flame abilities. > Umbral + Affectionate An Umbral Entheon may be extremely affectionate toward its trainer. > Radiant + Mischievous A Radiant Entheon may enjoy playing harmless tricks. > Dragon + Shy A Dragon Entheon may be hesitant and reserved around unfamiliar people. > Mystic + Competitive A Mystic Entheon may actively seek out challenging opponents. Unexpected combinations are allowed and can create memorable individuals.

9. Nature and Species Development

Nature should generally remain consistent throughout an Entheon’s life, including evolution. However, Personality Expression can develop through experience. An Entheon that begins as: > Nature: Timid >  Personality: Shy, Cautious may gradually become: > Nature: Timid >  Personality: Protective, Determined after developing a strong bond with its trainer. The Entheon remains fundamentally cautious, but experience has changed how that Nature manifests. Evolution may also influence personality expression without automatically changing Nature.

10. Individual Personality Generation

  1. The Entheon’s species description.
  2. Its established species behavior.
  3. Its Affinity.
  4. Its evolutionary stage.
  5. Its rarity.
  6. Its Nature.
  7. Its Personality Expressions.
  8. Its Individual Quirks.
  9. Its experiences.
  10. Its relationship with its trainer and other Entheon. Species and Affinity should provide context, while Nature and individual experience provide the primary foundation for personality.

11. Personality Is Not Guaranteed by Species

There should be no rule stating: > “All Elvarin are dignified.” or: > “All Brinderv are gentle.” Instead, the species description establishes what is typical or aesthetically appropriate, while individual Entheon can deviate from that baseline. For example, an Elvarin may be: - Dignified and serene. - Playful and mischievous. - Shy and reserved. - Proud and competitive. - Affectionate and devoted. - Completely oblivious to how captivating others find it. All remain valid Elvarin personalities.

12. Personality and Relationships

Personality should influence how Entheon interact with: - Their trainer. - Other Entheon. - Wild Entheon. - NPCs. - Environments. - Training. - Battles. - New experiences. However, personality should not override established commands or narrative circumstances automatically. A stubborn Entheon can still follow a trusted trainer’s command. A playful Entheon can become serious during a dangerous situation. A timid Entheon can act courageously when its trainer or companion is threatened. A proud Entheon can accept help from someone it trusts. Personality creates behavioral tendencies, not absolute restrictions.

13. Nature and Battle Behavior

Nature may influence how an Entheon behaves during battle, but should not directly modify its combat statistics. For example: - A Competitive Entheon may actively seek challenging opponents. - A Cautious Entheon may favor defensive positioning. - A Fierce Entheon may readily confront threats. - A Clever Entheon may experiment with unconventional tactics. - A Protective Entheon may prioritize defending an ally. - A Timid Entheon may initially favor distance or evasive tactics. These are behavioral tendencies rather than hidden statistical modifiers.

15. Core Design Principle

Nature provides the foundation. Personality provides the character. Quirks provide individuality. Experience provides development.

An Entheon should therefore never feel like: > “It is a Flame Entheon, so it is aggressive.” Instead, it should feel like: > “It is a Flame Entheon with a Timid Nature, an affectionate personality and an embarrassing habit of hiding behind its trainer whenever someone compliments it.” The species determines what it is. The Nature influences how it tends to behave. The Personality determines who this individual is. The Quirks make it memorable. The Entheon’s experiences determine who it may eventually become.
