Item System

1. Purpose

The Item System defines the physical items that exist within the creature world. Items are part of the world’s established setting and may be referenced by the creature encyclopedia, story events, trainers, shops, laboratories, researchers and other world elements. This document defines what items exist and what they do.

2. Item Categories

The world uses the following major item categories: 1. Capture Devices 2. Evolution Items 3. Healing and Recovery Items 4. Status Recovery Items 5. Battle Items 6. Training Items 7. Held Items 8. Key Items and Special Artifacts Not every category needs hundreds of individual items. Items should have clear, distinct purposes so that the world remains understandable and manageable.

3. Capture Devices

Capture devices are small crystalline devices used to contain and transport creatures. They replace the traditional “Capture Crystal” concept while functioning similarly at a mechanical level. A Capture Crystal temporarily converts a creature into a contained energy state and stores that creature within the crystal. When released, the creature returns to its physical form.

3.1 Canonical Physical Design

All Capture Crystals share the same fundamental physical design. A Capture Crystal is a slender, elongated, faceted crystal, resembling a polished gemstone shard with a distinctive tapered silhouette. The basic silhouette remains consistent across all grades of Capture Crystal. Higher-grade crystals are not fundamentally different objects; they are smaller, more refined versions of the same design. The crystal itself is the recognizable visual identity of the capture system.

Size by Grade

Device Approximate Crystal Size Typical Configuration
Capture Crystal Approximately the length of an adult male middle finger Necklace
Refined Crystal Noticeably smaller than a standard Capture Crystal Necklace, bracelet or compact mount
Primal Crystal Small enough for discreet personal mounting Bracelet, necklace or equipment mount
Sovereign Crystal Approximately one-third the length of a standard Capture Crystal Bracelet, pendant or specialized mount

These dimensions are approximate rather than exact measurements. The important distinction is that higher-grade Capture Crystals are progressively smaller while retaining the same fundamental shape.

3.2 Mounting and Wearing

The standard and most common way to carry a Capture Crystal is as a necklace or pendant. A standard Capture Crystal is relatively large, making it impractical or cumbersome for most bracelet configurations. Smaller, higher-grade crystals allow for greater freedom in how they are carried. Common configurations include: - Necklace or pendant. - Bracelet-mounted crystal. - Belt-mounted crystal. - Equipment-mounted crystal. - Specialized custom mounting. The mounting itself does not affect capture performance. The choice of mounting is primarily determined by: - Personal preference. - Comfort. - Fashion. - Profession. - Equipment requirements. - Available craftsmanship. - Crystal size. A trainer who specifically wants a bracelet-mounted Capture Crystal will generally need to invest in a smaller, higher-grade crystal or a specialized mounting solution. The necklace configuration remains the ordinary and most widespread form.

3.3 Visual Consistency

Despite differences in size, craftsmanship and mounting, all Capture Crystals should remain immediately recognizable as the same type of device. Manufacturers and individuals may vary: - The exact crystal cut. - Facet arrangement. - Mounting hardware. - Decorative elements. - Chain, cord or bracelet design. - Minor casing details. - Subtle color accents. However, the underlying slender, elongated, faceted crystal silhouette must remain consistent. The device should never resemble a spherical capsule or another unrelated containment device.

4. Capture Device Grades

4.1 Capture Crystal

Purpose: Standard capture device. The basic and most commonly available capture device. A standard Capture Crystal is approximately the length of an adult male middle finger and is most commonly worn as a necklace or pendant. Suitable for ordinary wild creatures and routine trainer use.

4.2 Refined Crystal

Purpose: Improved capture device. A more sophisticated and smaller version of the standard Capture Crystal, featuring a stronger containment field and more refined crystal construction. It provides a higher capture probability than a standard Capture Crystal, particularly against stronger or more resistant creatures. Its reduced size makes bracelet-mounted and other compact configurations more practical.

4.3 Primal Crystal

Purpose: High-grade capture device. A powerful and comparatively expensive Capture Crystal designed for difficult captures. Its highly refined crystal structure allows it to maintain a stronger containment field while remaining small enough for discreet personal mounting. Primal Crystals are uncommon compared with ordinary Capture Crystals.

4.4 Sovereign Crystal

Purpose: Exceptional capture device. An extremely rare and highly refined Capture Crystal. A Sovereign Crystal is approximately one-third the length of a standard Capture Crystal while retaining the same fundamental elongated, faceted design. Its compact size makes it particularly suitable for bracelet-mounted, pendant or other specialized configurations. A Sovereign Crystal is capable of attempting containment against creatures that would normally resist lesser devices. Sovereign Crystals are not ordinary equipment. Their rarity and cost make them unsuitable for routine use. A Sovereign Crystal does not guarantee successful capture.

5. Capture Rules

Capture devices do not automatically make a captured creature obedient. Successful capture establishes a containment and ownership relationship, but the creature’s: - Nature - Personality - Trust - Bond - Previous experiences - Individual temperament still influence its relationship with its trainer. A captured creature may therefore be: - Cooperative. - Cautious. - Distrustful. - Excited. - Resistant. - Deeply loyal. Capture is the beginning of a trainer-creature relationship, not a replacement for that relationship.

6. Capture Difficulty

Capture probability can be influenced by: - The creature’s current condition. - Its Level. - Its relative strength. - Its rarity. - Its temperament. - Whether it is willing to be captured. - The quality of the capture device. - Special circumstances surrounding the encounter. A weakened creature is generally easier to contain than a healthy creature at full strength. Extremely powerful or exceptionally rare creatures may resist even high-grade capture devices. Legendary creatures are not automatically capturable merely because a sufficiently powerful device exists.

7. Evolution Items

Evolution items are specialized objects that can trigger or enable specific evolutionary transformations. They are not generic power-up items. Each evolution item has a defined purpose and should only trigger an evolution when the appropriate species and conditions are present.

7.1 Storm Shard

Associated Evolution: Tivvi → Tivarra

Requirement:

Storm Shard The Storm Shard is a crystalline fragment charged with concentrated Volt energy. When used on an eligible Tivvi, it resonates with Tivvi’s natural Volt affinity and enables its evolution into Tivarra.

7.2 Emberstone

Associated Evolution: Veyli → Veylith

Requirement:

Level 28 + Emberstone An Emberstone is a warm, fire-charged evolutionary stone. It resonates with Flame-affinity energy and directs Veyli’s branching evolution toward Veylith.

7.3 Tide Pearl

Associated Evolution: Veyli → Veyrune

Requirement:

Level 28 + Tide Pearl A Tide Pearl is a naturally formed pearl containing concentrated Tide energy. It directs Veyli’s branching evolution toward Veyrune.

7.4 Mystic Crystal

Associated Evolution: Veyli → Veyvara

Requirement:

Level 28 + Mystic Crystal A Mystic Crystal contains concentrated Mystic energy. It directs Veyli’s branching evolution toward Veyvara.

7.5 Ancient Relic

Associated Evolution: Orli → Orlith

Requirement:

Level 30 + Ancient Relic The Ancient Relic is an old artifact containing residual energy from a previous age. Its resonance is specifically compatible with Orli’s artifact-like nature and enables its evolution into Orlith.

8. Evolution Items Are Species-Specific

Evolution items do not function as universal evolution triggers. For example: > An Emberstone does not cause every Flame-affinity creature to evolve. The species database determines which Entheon, if any, can evolve through a particular item. The item system never introduces an additional species-item relationship unless it has been explicitly established.

9. Healing and Recovery Items

Healing items restore a creature’s physical condition. The item system uses a small number of clearly differentiated recovery items rather than dozens of nearly identical products.

9.1 Basic Restore

A basic restorative item used to treat minor injuries and restore a limited amount of physical stamina. Suitable for routine field use.

9.2 Greater Restore

A stronger restorative item capable of treating more serious injuries and restoring significantly more stamina. More expensive than Basic Restore.

9.3 Full Restore

A high-grade restorative capable of restoring a creature to excellent physical condition after severe injury. Expensive and generally reserved for important situations.

10. Status Recovery Items

Status conditions can be treated using specialized restorative items.

10.1 Purity Tonic

A general-purpose treatment for common harmful status conditions.

10.2 Cleansing Elixir

A stronger treatment capable of removing more persistent or severe status effects.

10.3 Revitalizing Elixir

A specialized restorative used when a creature is exhausted or suffering from a debilitating condition. These items are intended as a separate category from ordinary healing.

11. Battle Items

Battle items temporarily influence combat performance. They may improve attributes such as: - Attack - Defense - Speed - Accuracy - Resistance - Affinity output Battle items provide temporary effects and do not permanently increase a creature’s underlying progression. They should be used sparingly during ordinary battles.

12. Training Items

Training items assist with development outside ordinary combat. Examples include: - Resistance-training equipment. - Affinity-control focuses. - Targeting devices. - Practice constructs. - Specialized training aids. Training equipment can make training more effective but does not bypass the ordinary progression system.

13. Held Items

Some items can be carried or worn by a creature and provide a persistent passive effect while equipped. Held items should be relatively uncommon and meaningful. Examples may include:

Item General Effect
Affinity Charm Slightly improves control of the user’s primary Affinity.
Vitality Band Improves stamina during prolonged activity.
Focus Lens Improves precision when using techniques requiring concentration.
Guard Charm Provides a minor defensive benefit.
Swift Band Provides a minor mobility benefit.

These are examples of the intended category and may be expanded later. Held items do not automatically replace a creature’s Special Move/Ability.

14. Key Items and Special Artifacts

Key Items are unique or limited objects associated with important locations, events, quests or creatures. Unlike ordinary consumables, they may exist primarily to: - Unlock locations. - Enable special interactions. - Trigger evolution. - Reveal information. - Resolve story situations. - Interact with ancient technology. - Interact with Affinity phenomena. Key Items should generally have a specific narrative purpose.

15. Bond Crystal

The Bond Crystal is a special item associated with the relationship between a trainer and their creature. It is not a capture device. A Bond Crystal is a personal crystal that represents the relationship between trainer and creature. It may be worn as: - A necklace. - A bracelet. - A belt-mounted crystal. - Another personal carrying arrangement. The Bond Crystal reacts to the emotional connection between trainer and creature. Its appearance and resonance may subtly change as the relationship develops. The Bond Crystal does not replace capture devices and cannot be used to capture creatures.

16. Item Rarity

Items may have different availability levels.

Common

Widely available everyday items. Examples: - Capture Crystals. - Basic healing items. - Basic training equipment.

Uncommon

More expensive or specialized items. Examples: - Refined Crystals. - Greater Restores. - Specialized training equipment.

Rare

Items that require significant resources, specialized knowledge or fortunate circumstances. Examples: - Primal Crystals. - Evolution items. - Advanced restorative items.

Extremely Rare

Items that are difficult to obtain and may be associated with major events. Examples: - Sovereign Crystals. - Unique ancient artifacts.

Unique

One-of-a-kind or extremely limited story objects. These should only exist when specifically established.

17. Item Acquisition

Items can be obtained through ordinary world activities such as: - Purchasing them from shops. - Receiving them from researchers or organizations. - Finding them in the wild. - Discovering them in ruins. - Receiving them as rewards. - Crafting them where appropriate. - Completing important tasks. - Trading with other people. Rare and unique items should generally require a meaningful reason or opportunity to obtain them.

18. Item Economy

Kaleo has an understandable item economy. Ordinary trainers should have reasonable access to: - Basic Capture Crystals. - Basic healing items. - Common training equipment. High-grade capture devices, evolution items and rare artifacts should be substantially more difficult to obtain. The existence of an item in the database does not mean every trainer automatically possesses one.

19. Item Naming Principle

The setting should use its own terminology. The following terms should not be used as canonical world terminology: - Poké Ball - Great Ball - Ultra Ball - Master Ball - Pokémon Center - Pokédex Kaleo uses setting-specific terminology for these concepts.

Canonical creature term:** **Entheon

Singular: Entheon   Plural: Entheon   Individual: an Entheon   Species: Entheon species The current canonical capture-device terminology is: > Capture Crystal → Refined Crystal → Primal Crystal → Sovereign Crystal The creature species themselves use Entheon as the established canonical term throughout the setting.

20. Expansion Rules

Additional items may be added to the database later. New items should: 1. Have a clearly defined purpose. 2. Belong to an established item category or justify a new category. 3. Have a distinct mechanical or narrative function. 4. Avoid duplicating an existing item’s role without a meaningful reason. 5. Be compatible with the established creature, Affinity, evolution and progression systems. 6. Not create new evolution paths unless those paths are explicitly added to the species database. The item database is intended to remain coherent and manageable rather than becoming an exhaustive catalogue of minor variations.
