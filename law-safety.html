17. Law, Safety & Public Rules

Kaleo is a modern society governed by established laws and institutions. Entheon are deeply integrated into everyday life, and the legal system therefore includes specific protections and regulations concerning Entheon, Trainers, Capture Crystals and Entheon-related activities. These rules exist alongside ordinary civilian law.

17.1 General Law

Kalean law covers the same broad areas expected in a modern society, including:

  • personal safety
  • property
  • contracts
  • fraud
  • theft
  • assault
  • public safety
  • environmental protection
  • commercial activity

Entheon do not exist outside the normal legal framework. Crimes committed using Entheon are still subject to ordinary criminal law, with additional considerations where the welfare or agency of an Entheon is involved.

17.2 Trainer Responsibilities

Registered Trainers are responsible for the Entheon under their care. This includes providing appropriate:

  • food
  • shelter
  • healthcare
  • exercise
  • enrichment
  • supervision

A Trainer is also responsible for taking reasonable precautions when using Entheon in public or allowing them to interact with other people and Entheon. Trainer registration and the associated TraID provide the legal foundation for recognizing Trainer and Entheon ownership.

17.3 Entheon Welfare and Protection

Entheon are recognized as sentient beings with individual agency. Their welfare is protected by law, including protections against unnecessary cruelty, neglect, exploitation and abuse. Entheon used for work, research, entertainment or battling remain entitled to appropriate care. The existence of an Entheon partnership does not remove the Entheon’s individual welfare considerations.

17.4 Capture and Ownership

Capture Crystals and Entheon ownership are regulated. Registered Capture Crystals are linked to their legal owner, and captured Entheon are associated with both the Crystal and their Trainer’s TraID. This system provides the foundation for:

  • ownership
  • transfers
  • trading
  • identifying stolen Entheon
  • investigating illegal captures

Unauthorized capture, theft, trafficking and deliberate attempts to circumvent the registration system are subject to legal consequences.

17.5 Public Battling

Entheon battles are a normal part of Kalean society. Registered Trainers may participate in roadside battles, friendly battles, Gym challenges, tournaments and other recognized forms of competition. Battles in public spaces must be conducted in a manner that does not unnecessarily endanger bystanders, property or uninvolved Entheon. A Trainer is not required to accept a battle challenge. Declining a challenge is not inherently suspicious or dishonorable.

17.6 Battle Safety

Entheon combat has an inherent limitation:

An Entheon cannot kill another Entheon through direct combat.

Battles can nevertheless result in knockouts, exhaustion, temporary injuries, serious non-lethal injuries and the need for medical treatment or recovery. This limitation is an inherent rule of Entheon combat rather than merely a regulation imposed by the League or other competitive organizations. Entheon can still die from illness, natural causes, accidents, environmental hazards and circumstances unrelated to direct Entheon combat.

17.7 Wild Entheon

Wild Entheon remain part of Kaleo’s natural ecosystems and are subject to environmental and conservation protections. Trainers and other individuals are expected to respect:

  • protected habitats
  • conservation areas
  • breeding populations
  • restricted ecological zones
  • local wildlife regulations

Interfering with wild Entheon populations, destroying habitats or capturing Entheon in prohibited circumstances may constitute a legal offense. Specific restrictions can vary according to the region and the ecological importance of an area.

17.8 Criminal Use of Entheon

Using Entheon to commit crimes is illegal. This can include:

  • assault
  • theft
  • intimidation
  • smuggling
  • illegal trafficking
  • property destruction
  • coercion
  • unauthorized capture or transfer

The fact that an Entheon is capable of causing significant damage does not place responsibility solely on the Entheon. Human actions and the circumstances surrounding the incident are considered when determining responsibility.

17.9 Rogue Trainers

A person who possesses or trains captured Entheon without maintaining the legally required Trainer registration may be referred to as a rogue Trainer. Being a rogue Trainer does not automatically make someone a criminal. However, rogue Trainers may attract additional scrutiny because unregistered Entheon can be associated with stolen Entheon, illegal trafficking, underground battling, concealed identities or deliberate avoidance of legal oversight. “Rogue Trainer” is primarily a common social term rather than necessarily a formal criminal classification.

17.10 Law Enforcement and Emergency Services

Kaleo has ordinary law enforcement and emergency services operating throughout its settlements. Depending on the situation, authorities may respond to incidents involving:

  • criminal activity
  • dangerous Entheon
  • accidents
  • missing persons
  • environmental emergencies
  • public safety threats
  • illegal capture or trafficking

Emergency medical services can treat both humans and Entheon, with specialized Entheon medical facilities available in larger settlements.

17.11 Public Safety and Responsibility

Advanced technology and the widespread presence of Entheon make public safety a shared responsibility. Individuals are expected to use appropriate judgment when:

  • bringing Entheon into public spaces
  • battling near other people
  • travelling through populated areas
  • interacting with unfamiliar Entheon
  • exploring wilderness
  • using specialized Affinity abilities or equipment

The law provides the framework for dealing with dangerous situations, but ordinary caution and responsible behaviour remain an important part of everyday life.

17.12 Relationship Between Trainer Institutions and Civilian Law

Gyms, research centres and League organizations operate within the broader Kalean legal system. They may establish additional rules governing their own activities, competitions or facilities, but these rules do not replace ordinary civilian law.
